The **Audit Log** records sensitive actions as they happen. Entries can never be edited or deleted, by anyone.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Open the Audit Log

Open the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) and choose **Go to Audit Log**.

<Frame caption="The Audit Log, newest first.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/audit-log.webp" alt="Audit Log listing when, performed by, action, category and record" />
</Frame>

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | Date and time. |
| **Performed by** | The person, or the system for automatic actions. |
| **Action** | What happened, for example *offer confirmed* or *attendance corrected*. |
| **Category** | attendance, manager, manager workspace, offer, organisation, role or user. |
| **Record** | The record affected. |

Filter by **Action**, **Category** or **Date**. The search box searches the current page.

## What is recorded

Examples include sign-ins and password changes, invitations sent, resent and cancelled, accounts suspended and reactivated, offers sent, accepted, confirmed, rejected and expired, automatic confirmations, clock-ins, clock-outs, corrections and finalisations, workspace changes, and platform administration actions.

## Retention

Audit entries are append-only: the database refuses any change or deletion, even from Crewflow's own services.
