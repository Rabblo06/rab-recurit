Crewflow produces two kinds of PDF for each shift. Both are A4 and are emailed to the managers linked to the venue.

| Document | When | Contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shift roster** | About 3 hours before the shift starts | The shift details, the confirmed team, and the shift's **QR code** for clocking in and out. |
| **Final timesheet** | Shortly after the venue manager selects **Finalise & send** | Each person's scheduled and actual times, breaks and worked hours, in two versions: **Original** (as recorded, "Unsigned") and **Venue signed** (after review). |

## View or download

In the web console, open the shift's [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline) and select **Timesheet Report**. Each document has **View** and **Download**. Download links are temporary and checked against your access each time.

## Printing

Open the PDF with **View** or **Download** and print it from your PDF viewer. The roster is designed to be printed so the QR code can be displayed at the venue.

## Exports

<Note>
  Crewflow does not export timesheets or attendance to CSV or spreadsheets. Use the PDFs, or the [Payroll](/payroll/reviewing-payroll) page for hours and pay.
</Note>
