A **venue manager** looks after one or more venues — for example, the events manager at a hotel. They use the mobile app in Venue manager mode.

## Invite a venue manager

- **Managers:** **Settings → Members → Invite** with the **Venue manager** role, or **Invite venue manager** in the account menu. The venue manager joins your workspace.
- **CEOs:** **Users → Managers → New manager**, choose **Venue manager** and **Select Venue**.

See [Manager accounts](/account/manager-accounts).

## What venue managers can do

| Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- |
| See the venues they are assigned to | See other venues or the rest of the workspace |
| Browse staff available to their venues and keep a team list | See staff pay rates or private details |
| Submit staffing requests with chosen staff, times and breaks | Send offers directly, or confirm and reject acceptances |
| Follow events: required, confirmed and open positions | Create or edit venues |
| Review attendance, correct times with a reason, and finalise timesheets | See payroll |
| | Sign in to the web console, or invite anyone |

## Available staff and favourites

In the mobile app, **All Users** lists staff available to the venue manager's venues, with "Available" or "Not Available" for the date in question. **Add staff** saves someone to the venue manager's **Users** list, which works as their go-to team.

<Note>
  There is no separate favourites feature. The saved **Users** team list is how venue managers keep their regular staff to hand.
</Note>
