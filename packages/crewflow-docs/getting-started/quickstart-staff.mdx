<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate your account">
    Your employer adds you to Crewflow and you receive an invitation email. Open it within 24 hours and choose a password of at least 10 characters, with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the app">
    Install the Crewflow app on your Android phone and sign in with your email and new password. You can then turn on fingerprint or face unlock. [First sign-in](/mobile/first-sign-in)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to offers">
    Tap **Open Shift Offers** on Home, open an offer to see the pay, time and location, then tap **Accept** or **Decline**. [Offers](/mobile/offers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    After you accept, the offer shows "Waiting for manager confirmation". Once confirmed, the shift appears under **Upcoming Shift** and in your **Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock in and out at the venue">
    From 15 minutes before your shift, tap **Clock in** on Home, allow location access and scan the shift QR code at the venue. Clock out the same way when you finish. [Clock in and out](/mobile/clock-in-and-out)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your completed shifts, with the hours worked and the amount earned, are in the **History** tab.
</Tip>
