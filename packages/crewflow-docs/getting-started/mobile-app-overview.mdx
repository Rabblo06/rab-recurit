The Crewflow mobile app is for the people who do the work and the people who host it. It runs on **Android**. An iOS version is not available yet.

## Two modes, one app

| | Staff mode | Venue manager mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who** | Staff | Venue managers |
| **Tabs** | Home, Calendar, History, Profile | Home, Calendar, History, Profile |
| **Main tasks** | Accept or decline offers, clock in and out, see upcoming and past shifts | Request staff, follow event staffing, review and finalise timesheets |

The app opens in the right mode for your account. Managers and CEOs who sign in to the app see a **Management console** screen with a button to open the web console instead.

## Signing in

You sign in with the email and password from your invitation. After your first password sign-in, the app offers to set up fingerprint or face unlock. Each time the app starts, it asks you to unlock with your fingerprint or face, or with your password if you skipped that setup.

[First sign-in](/mobile/first-sign-in) · [Biometric sign-in](/mobile/biometric-sign-in)

## Staying up to date

The app refreshes your offers and attendance every 30 seconds and whenever you return to it. Notifications appear in **Profile → Inbox** and on the bell on Home.

<Note>
  Push notifications on the lock screen are still being set up. Open the app to see new offers. See [Product status](/getting-started/product-status).
</Note>
