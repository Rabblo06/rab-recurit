<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    Open the invitation email, choose a password and sign in at [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace and profile">
    Name your workspace, choose its web address, then add your first and last name. Crewflow prepares the workspace and welcomes you.
    <VideoTutorial src="/videos/managers/create-workspace.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/create-workspace.webp" title="Creating your workspace" duration="Under 1 minute" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your venues">
    Open **Venues → New venue**. Add the address, and optionally the map location and geofence so staff must be on site to clock in. [Venues](/venues/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your staff">
    Open **Users → New staff** and complete the four steps. Each person receives an invitation email to set up the mobile app. [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a shift and send offers">
    Open **Shifts → New shift**, choose the venue, role, date and times, select the staff to invite, and select **Send Offer**. [Creating shifts](/shifts/creating-shifts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the people who accept">
    Watch **Offers**. When staff accept, confirm them individually with **Confirm shift**, or all at once with **Confirm All Accepted**. [Confirming offers](/shifts/confirming-offers)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You do not have to confirm immediately. If you take no action for 60 minutes after someone accepts, Crewflow confirms them automatically and tells you.
</Tip>

## Your daily routine

| When | Where | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | **Dashboard** | Active offers and offers awaiting confirmation. |
| Before shifts | **Calendar** | Today's shifts and how many staff are allocated. |
| During shifts | **Offers → Venue Offers → live staffing** | Late staff, clock-ins and clock-outs. |
| After shifts | **Payroll** | Hours and calculated pay. |
