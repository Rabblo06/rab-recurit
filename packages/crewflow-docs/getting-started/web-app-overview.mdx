The web console is where managers and CEOs run their workspace. Open it at [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk) and sign in with your work email.

## The sidebar

The sidebar has three modes, switched at the top: **Home**, **AI** and **Settings**.

In **Home** mode, the **Workspace** section lists:

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Totals, offers by status, staff overview and recent offers. |
| **Users** | Staff and managers. Add people, manage invitations and edit records. |
| **Shifts** | Every shift in your workspace, with filters and actions. |
| **Offers** | Staff offers and venue manager requests. |
| **Calendar** | A month view of all shifts. |
| **Payroll** | Hours and calculated pay from attendance. |
| **Venues** | Your venues, their location settings and pay rates. |

At the bottom of the sidebar, the **Priority** card shows unread updates, and your name opens the account menu with **Theme**, **Settings** and, depending on your role, **New Manager** and **Invite venue manager**.

## The top bar

- **Breadcrumb** — shows where you are, for example *Workspace / Shifts*.
- **Ask AI** — opens the AI panel. No AI service is connected yet, so sending is disabled.
- **Bell** — your notifications.
- **⋮** — opens the command palette.

## Search

Press <kbd>/</kbd> to search across staff, managers, shifts, offers and venues. Results appear as you type.

## List pages

Users, Shifts, Offers, Venues and Payroll share the same tools:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Views and tabs" icon="table-list">
    Tabs and the view menu switch between saved filters, such as **Upcoming** shifts or offers **Awaiting Confirmation**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search, Filter and Sort" icon="filter">
    Search narrows the list. **Filter** adds conditions such as venue or status. **Sort** changes the order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Options" icon="sliders">
    Show or hide columns, and reset them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Paging" icon="file-lines">
    The footer shows the number of records and pages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If a list cannot load, Crewflow says "We couldn't load these records. Your data has not been changed." and offers **Retry**. An empty list is never shown in place of an error.
</Tip>

## Audit Log

The Audit Log is not in the sidebar. Open it from the command palette with **Go to Audit Log**. See [Audit log](/payroll/audit-log).
