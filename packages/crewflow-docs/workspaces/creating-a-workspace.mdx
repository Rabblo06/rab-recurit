## Who can use this

Internal managers and CEOs, the first time they sign in after accepting their invitation. Crewflow takes you to these screens automatically.

<VideoTutorial src="/videos/managers/create-workspace.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/create-workspace.webp" title="Creating a workspace and profile" duration="About 1 minute" />

## Create your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a logo (optional)">
    Select **Upload logo** and choose a PNG, JPG or WEBP image up to 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Enter the **Workspace name**, for example your branch or team name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the web address">
    **Workspace address** fills in from the name. Crewflow checks it as you type and shows "✓ … is available" when it can be used. If it is taken, pick one of the suggestions or type another. After editing it yourself, **↺ Regenerate from name** puts the suggestion back.
    <Frame caption="Create your workspace, with the address confirmed as available.">
      <img src="/images/web/getting-started/create-workspace.webp" alt="Create your workspace form with workspace name and an available workspace address" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create workspace" />
</Steps>

## Create your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your details">
    Enter your **First name** and **Last name**, an optional **Job title**, and optionally **Upload photo**.
    <Frame caption="Create your profile — your name is how your team will see you.">
      <img src="/images/web/getting-started/create-profile.webp" alt="Create your profile form with first name, last name and job title" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Complete setup" />
</Steps>

## Preparing your workspace

Crewflow now builds your workspace. The **Preparing your workspace** screen ticks through *Setting up your workspace*, *Getting everything in place* and *Making it feel like home*. It takes at least a few seconds.

<Frame caption="Crewflow only opens your workspace once it is genuinely ready.">
  <img src="/images/web/getting-started/preparing-workspace.webp" alt="Preparing your workspace screen with a three-step checklist" />
</Frame>

When it is ready you see a welcome — "*Workspace name* is ready" — and then your Dashboard.

## What happens next

Your workspace is empty and ready. Add [venues](/venues/overview) and [staff](/staff/adding-staff) next.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Workspace name is required." / "Please enter a subdomain." / "Enter a valid subdomain." | Fill in the highlighted field. Addresses use lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens. |
| "This subdomain is already taken." or "This subdomain cannot be used." | Choose a suggestion or a different address. |
| "Couldn't check availability. Please try again." | Your connection dropped. Try again. |
| "Still preparing your workspace" | It is taking longer than usual. Select **Check again**, or **Sign out** and return later. |
| "We hit a snag preparing your workspace" | Preparation failed. Contact your Crewflow administrator. |

<Warning>
  Once setup is complete, using the browser's Back button to return to these setup screens signs you out, to stop an old form being submitted again. Sign in again to continue.
</Warning>
