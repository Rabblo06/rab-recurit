## Deactivate a staff member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    In **Users → Staff**, hover over the person to show the row buttons, or open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Deactivate">
    Crewflow sets their employment status to **inactive** and suspends their account. They are signed out of the mobile app on every device and cannot sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deactivated staff are not offered new shifts. Their past shifts, attendance and pay stay unchanged. Select **Reactivate** to set them back to **active** and restore access with their existing password.

<Note>
  Deactivate and Reactivate only appear once the person has accepted their invitation. For someone still invited, use **Cancel invitation** or **Resend invitation**.
</Note>

## Delete an account

**Delete user** is for accounts created in error.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open More actions">
    On the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete user">
    Type **DELETE** to confirm and enter your current password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You cannot delete someone who has worked shifts or has other history. Crewflow shows "This user has historical workforce records and cannot be permanently deleted. Suspend the account instead." Deactivate them instead.
</Warning>

## Bulk actions

You can select several people and send them an email — see [Bulk email](/staff/bulk-email). Bulk deactivation, bulk deletion and export are not available; change accounts one at a time.
