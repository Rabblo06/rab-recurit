icon: "screwdriver-wrench"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in" icon="right-to-bracket">
    Check your email and password, then tap **Forgot password?** if needed. "Unable to reach the server" means no connection. If you never accepted your invitation email, ask your manager to resend it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app asks for my password every time" icon="fingerprint">
    Turn on biometric sign-in in **Profile → Security**. Every 90 days you must still sign in with your password once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see an offer my manager sent" icon="paper-plane">
    Close and reopen the app, or wait 30 seconds — offers refresh automatically. Lock-screen alerts are not available yet. If it still does not appear, the offer may have expired or been withdrawn.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I accepted but the shift isn't in my calendar" icon="calendar">
    It shows "Awaiting confirmation" until a manager confirms you. This normally happens within an hour.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clock in doesn't work" icon="qrcode">
    Clock-in opens 15 minutes before the start. Make sure you scan this shift's QR code, that location and camera are allowed, and that you are at the venue. See [Clock in and out](/mobile/clock-in-and-out).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My hours look wrong" icon="clock">
    Tell your venue manager before they finalise the timesheet. They can correct clock-in, clock-out and break times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was signed out unexpectedly" icon="right-from-bracket">
    Your password may have been reset, your account paused, or you have not used the app for 7 days. Sign in again, or contact your manager.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no app for my iPhone" icon="apple">
    Crewflow is currently available on Android only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
