## The four tabs

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See today's or your next event, open positions and upcoming events; reach Sent offers, Users, Confirmed staff and Reports from **My Space**. |
| **Calendar** | See every event at your venues and open **Event Details**. |
| **History** | Review past events and confirmed staffing. |
| **Profile** | Notifications, biometric sign-in and **Log out**. |

## Home

**organization** view:

- **Today's Event** or **Next Event**, with "Staff joined: X / Y"
- **Open positions** — "N staff needed" across your assigned venues
- **Report** — shortcut to timesheets
- **Upcoming Event** — your next events

**My Space** view:

- **My venues** — the venues you look after
- **Overview** — **Sent offers**, **Users**, **Confirmed staff**, **Upcoming Events** and **Reports**

## What you can and cannot do

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="You can" icon="circle-check">
    Request staff with chosen times and breaks, keep a team list, follow staffing live, correct attendance with a reason, and finalise timesheets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You cannot" icon="circle-xmark">
    See other venues, see pay rates, confirm or reject staff acceptances, see payroll, or use the web console.
  </Card>
</Columns>
