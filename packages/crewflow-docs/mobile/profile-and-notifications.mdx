## Profile

**Profile** shows your name and email, and three options:

| Option | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Opens your notifications. A badge shows how many are unread. |
| **Security** | Turns biometric sign-in on or off. See [Biometric sign-in](/mobile/biometric-sign-in). |
| **Log out** | Signs you out of this phone straight away. Your email and biometric setting are remembered for next time. |

To change your name, phone number or other details, ask your manager.

## Notifications

Open them from **Profile → Inbox** or the bell on Home. Tap a notification to mark it read, or **Mark all read**. "No notifications yet." means there is nothing new.

You are told about new offers, confirmations, rejections, reminders before your shift (24 hours, 2 hours and 30 minutes) and when clock-in opens.

<Note>
  Notifications appear inside the app. Lock-screen push notifications are not available yet, so open the app regularly.
</Note>
