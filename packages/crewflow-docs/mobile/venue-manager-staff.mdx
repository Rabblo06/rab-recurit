## Users and All Users

| Screen | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Users** | Your saved team — the people you work with regularly. |
| **All Users** | Everyone available to your venues. Tap **Add staff** to save someone to your team; they then show **Added**. |

Open them from **My Space → Users**.

## Search and filter

Use **Search User**, and filter by **Employment status**: All, Active, Pending compliance, Inactive or Suspended.

## Badges

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **NEW** | Joined in the last 7 days. |
| **Available** / **Not Available** | Whether they can take the shift you are planning. |

Tap someone for **Staff details** — "Visible through your assigned venues." You see what you need to staff an event, never pay rates or private information.

<Note>
  There is no favourites list. Your **Users** team is the list of people you prefer to work with, and it is the quickest place to pick from in **Select Staff**.
</Note>
