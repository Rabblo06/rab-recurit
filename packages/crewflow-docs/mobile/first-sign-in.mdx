## Before you begin

- You have accepted your invitation email and chosen a password. See [Accepting an invitation](/account/accepting-an-invitation).
- The Crewflow app is installed on your Android phone.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    The first time, three welcome pages introduce the app. Tap **Get Started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    On **Welcome back** — "Log in to see your shifts." — enter your **EMAIL** and **PASSWORD**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign In" />
  <Step title="Set up biometric unlock (optional)">
    The app offers **Set up Biometric Login**. Tap **Enable Passkey** to use your fingerprint or face next time, or **Skip for now**. See [Biometric sign-in](/mobile/biometric-sign-in).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The app opens in the right mode for your account — Staff or Venue manager. You never choose.

## If you are asked to set a new password

If your manager reset your password, the app opens **Set secure password**. Enter it under **CREATE PASSWORD** and **CONFIRM PASSWORD**. It needs 10 or more characters with upper-case and lower-case letters and a number, and must not be a password you have used before. After "Password updated successfully", tap **Go back to login** and sign in with the new password.

## Common errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Invalid email or password." | Check your details or tap **Forgot password?**. |
| "Unable to reach the server. Check your connection and try again." | Check Wi-Fi or mobile data. |
| "Too many sign-in attempts. Please try again later." | Wait, then try again. The button is disabled until you can. |
| "Account access — This account has no supported mobile role." | Your account is not a staff or venue manager account. Contact your manager. |
| "Management console" | You are a manager. Tap **Open management console** to use the web console. |
