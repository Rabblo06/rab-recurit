| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See today's or your next shift, open offers, clock in and out, and your upcoming shifts. |
| **Calendar** | See confirmed shifts and open offers by day or month. |
| **History** | Review completed shifts, hours and earnings. |
| **Profile** | Read notifications (**Inbox**), set up biometric unlock (**Security**) and **Log out**. |

## Keeping up to date

The app refreshes offers and attendance every 30 seconds and whenever you return to it. To refresh straight away, pull down on the screen. Lock-screen push notifications are not available yet, so open the app to check for new offers.
