A shift request asks your Crewflow manager for staff. You choose who you would like; the manager approves, and offers then go to those people.

## Send a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Send Shift">
    On Home, switch to **My Space** and tap **Sent offers**, then tap **+**. **Send Shift** — "Create and send a shift offer to staff".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the venue and role">
    **Venue** and **Role** are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and times">
    **Date** (tomorrow by default), **Start time** and **End time** (09:00–17:00 by default). An overnight shift shows "(+1 day)".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the break">
    **Break (minutes)**. Leave it blank to use the venue's default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select staff">
    Tap **Select staff**, choose people and tap **Submit**. **Number of staff required** shows how many you selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust individual times (optional)">
    Tap a person's time to give them their own **Start time**, **End time** or **Break (minutes) (Optional)** — useful for staggered starts. Tap **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Send Shift Offer">
    "Shift submitted successfully — Your staffing request has been sent for manager approval. You can track its status from your shifts." Tap **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

1. Your manager reviews the request and can add or remove people. You are told if someone is removed.
2. When approved, offers go to the selected staff.
3. When staff accept, they are confirmed automatically.
4. Track progress in **Sent Shifts**.

## Track your requests

**Sent Shifts** — "Track submitted staffing requests" — shows totals for **Sent**, **Accepted** and **Confirmed**, and a card per request with "N staff required" and "X accepted · Y confirmed". Search with **Search shifts…** or filter by status. It refreshes every few seconds.

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Choose a venue" / "Choose a role" | Fill in both. |
| "Select at least one staff member." | Select staff before sending. |
| "Break must be shorter than the shift." | Reduce the break. |
| "End must follow start; break must be a whole number shorter than the assignment." | Check a person's individual times. |
| "The send result could not be confirmed. Check Sent Shifts before creating another shift." | Your connection dropped. Check **Sent Shifts** so you do not send twice. |
| "Your account does not have permission to submit shift requests." | Contact your Crewflow manager. |
