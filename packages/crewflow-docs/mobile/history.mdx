Each card shows:

- the venue and role
- what you earned for the shift
- its status
- the date
- **Scheduled** times and **Actual** times, with hours worked — for example "Actual 16:58–23:04 · 5h 36m worked"

Your hours are worked out from your clock-in and clock-out times minus your break. If your venue manager corrected a time, the corrected time is shown.

"No history yet — Completed shifts will show up here." means you have not completed a shift yet. If history cannot load, tap **Retry**.

<Note>
  Earnings are calculated gross amounts before tax. Crewflow does not pay you directly — your employer pays you through their payroll.
</Note>
