## Start with your organisation

Most questions are answered fastest by someone in your own organisation:

| You are | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff** | The manager who invited you, or the venue manager on site. |
| **Venue manager** | The Crewflow manager who invited you. |
| **Manager** | Your CEO or Crewflow administrator. |
| **CEO** | Your Crewflow account contact at {{company}}. |

## Before you get in touch

Have these ready:

- your name and the email address on your account
- which app you were using (web console or mobile app) and your phone model if mobile
- what you were trying to do and the exact message you saw
- the date and time it happened

<Warning>
  Never send your password, a sign-in link or a reset link to anyone, including support.
</Warning>

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, report it privately through the repository's [security policy](https://github.com/Rabblo06/rab-recurit/security/policy). Please do not report it publicly.
