Staff answer offers in the mobile app. As a manager, you see their answer on the **Offers** page and in your notifications.

## Accepting

The staff member opens the offer and taps **Accept**. The offer becomes **staff accepted** and shows "Waiting for manager confirmation" in the app and **Awaiting Confirmation** on your Offers page.

Acceptance only succeeds if the offer is still pending and has not passed its deadline. If two things happen at once — for example you withdraw an offer as they tap Accept — only one of them wins.

## Declining

The staff member taps **Decline**. The offer becomes **declined** and cannot be reopened. You are notified ("A staff member declines my offer") and Crewflow starts looking for a replacement.

<Note>
  **Decline reasons:** the staff app does not ask for a reason when declining. Reasons appear on the Offers page only when a manager rejects an acceptance and gives one.
</Note>

## No answer

If the staff member does nothing, the offer expires at its deadline (48 hours after sending, by default). See [Offer expiry](/shifts/offer-expiry).

## After the manager decides

| Manager action | Staff member sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm shift** | **CONFIRMED**. The shift appears under Upcoming Shift and in their calendar. |
| **Reject** | The offer ends. It no longer appears as an upcoming shift. |
| No action within 60 minutes | Crewflow confirms automatically. |

See the staff side in detail in [Offers (mobile)](/mobile/offers).
