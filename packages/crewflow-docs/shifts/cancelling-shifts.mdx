## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. Any shift that has not finished can be cancelled.

## Cancel a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the shift">
    In **Shifts**, hover over the shift and select the **Cancel shift** button (the circle-slash icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Enter the **Reason for cancellation** — for example "Venue cancelled the booking".
    <Frame caption="Cancel shift asks for a reason, which is kept with the shift.">
      <img src="/images/web/shifts/cancel-shift-drawer.webp" alt="Cancel shift drawer with a reason for cancellation field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Cancel shift">
    Select **Back** if you change your mind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to offers and staff

Within about a minute, Crewflow tidies up everything attached to the shift:

| Before cancellation | After |
| --- | --- |
| Pending offers | **Withdrawn** — staff can no longer accept them. |
| Accepted, unconfirmed offers | **Rejected**, with the reason "This shift was cancelled." |
| Confirmed places | **Cancelled**. |
| Open replacement searches | Stopped. |

Everyone affected receives a **shift cancelled** notification. Crewflow records who cancelled the shift, when, and why.

<Warning>
  Cancelling cannot be undone. To run the shift after all, create a new one.
</Warning>

## Cancelling one person instead

To remove one person but keep the shift, use **Withdraw offer** on the Offers page for a pending offer, or **Cancel offer** on the [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline) up to 15 minutes before the start.
