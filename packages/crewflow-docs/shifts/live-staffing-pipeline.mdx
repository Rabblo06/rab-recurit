Each approved venue request has a **live staffing** board that shows where every person is, from the moment their offer is sent until they clock out. It refreshes every few seconds.

## Open the board

Go to **Offers → Venue Offers** and select a request's row to open its board. **← Venue Offers** takes you back.

## Summary

Across the top: **Required**, **Confirmed**, **Offered**, **Waiting**, **Accepted**, **Late**, **Open**, **Rejected**, **Clocked in** and **Clocked out**.

## Columns

| Column | A person is here when… |
| --- | --- |
| **OFFERED** | Their offer was sent and they have not opened it yet. |
| **WAITING** | They have seen the offer but not answered. |
| **STAFF ACCEPTED** | They accepted, and are confirmed or awaiting confirmation. |
| **LATE STAFF** | They are confirmed but have not clocked in 10 minutes after the start. |
| **CLOCKED IN** | They have clocked in. |
| **CLOCKED OUT** | They have clocked out. |
| **DELETED OFFER** | The offer was declined, expired, withdrawn or rejected, or the shift was cancelled. |

## Card actions

Open the menu on a person's card for **View staff**, **Notes** and **Cancel offer**.

**Cancel offer** asks "Cancel *name*'s offer?" with **Keep Staff** or **Cancel Offer**. You can cancel up to **15 minutes before the shift starts**, as long as the person has not clocked in.

## Fill gaps

Select **+ Select replacement staff** to open **Select Staff** in replacement mode. Choose people and select **Send offers**. They join the board in **OFFERED**.

## Timesheets

Select **Timesheet Report** to open the shift's timesheets:

| Document | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Timesheet** | The times as recorded, before review. Marked "Unsigned". |
| **Venue Signed Timesheet** | The version after the venue manager reviewed and finalised it. |

Each has **View** and **Download**.
