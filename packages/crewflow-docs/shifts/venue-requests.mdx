Venue managers ask for staff from the mobile app's **Send Shift** screen. Their request reaches you as a shift with the status **Pending approval**. Nothing is sent to staff until you approve it.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs who can approve staffing requests.

<VideoTutorial src="/videos/offers/venue-request-approval.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/venue-request-approval.webp" title="Approving a venue manager's request" duration="About 1 minute" />

## Find requests

Open **Offers** and select the **Venue Offers** tab. You are also notified when a request is submitted.

<Frame caption="Offers → Venue Offers lists every request with its staffing and status.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/venue-offers-list.webp" alt="Venue Offers list with venue, role, venue manager, date, staff selected and status" />
</Frame>

Views: **All requests**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Declined**. The **Staff** column shows how many people were selected against how many are required.

## Review and approve

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Select a pending request to open the **Shift Approval** drawer. It shows the role, date, time, rate, break, staffing ("x selected / y required"), the venue manager's note and the **Selected staff**.
    <Frame caption="The Shift Approval drawer.">
      <img src="/images/web/offers/shift-approval-drawer.webp" alt="Shift approval drawer with staffing summary, note and selected staff list" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the staff (optional)">
    Remove anyone who should not be offered the shift — people marked "No longer active" or "Unavailable for shift" can't be offered. Select **Select N Staff** to open the **Select Staff** page and add people.
    <Frame caption="Select Staff: choose people to fill the remaining places.">
      <img src="/images/web/offers/select-staff-page.webp" alt="Select Staff page listing staff with name, reference, email, rate and status" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Select **Approve**. Offers go to the selected staff. When they accept, they are **confirmed automatically**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decline a request

Select **Decline**, optionally add a **Reason (optional)**, and **Confirm decline**. The venue manager is told the request was declined.

## Notifications

| Event | Who is told |
| --- | --- |
| Request submitted | Managers who approve requests |
| Request approved or declined | The venue manager who sent it |
| You removed someone from the selection | The venue manager, so they know their choice changed |

## After approval

Follow each person from offer to clock-out on the [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).
