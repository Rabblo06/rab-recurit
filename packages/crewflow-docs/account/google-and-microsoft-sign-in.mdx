Crewflow supports signing in with Google or Microsoft as an alternative to a password. These options only appear when they are switched on for both the Crewflow service you use and your organisation.

## How it works

- **Invitation first.** Google and Microsoft sign-in never create a Crewflow account. You must already have been invited, and the email on your Google or Microsoft account must match your Crewflow email.
- **Verified emails only.** For Google, the email must be verified by Google.
- **Same session rules.** You stay signed in under the same timeouts as password sign-in.

## Sign in with Google or Microsoft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the provider">
    Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** and follow the provider's screens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Crewflow">
    You come back to Crewflow signed in. If your account still needs a password set, Crewflow asks you to set one first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something goes wrong

If sign-in does not complete, Crewflow returns you to the sign-in page with "Try signing in again". The usual causes are:

- the provider account's email does not match your Crewflow email
- your organisation has not enabled that provider
- you cancelled at the provider's screen

<Note>
  The mobile app uses email and password, plus fingerprint or face unlock. Google and Microsoft sign-in are web console features.
</Note>
