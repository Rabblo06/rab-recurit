Open **Settings** from the sidebar mode switcher or the account menu. The **Your account** section has three pages.

## Profile

<Frame caption="Settings → Profile: photo, personal details, email, password and active sessions.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/settings-profile.webp" alt="Settings Profile page with profile photo, personal details and active sessions" />
</Frame>

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | Upload a PNG, JPG or WEBP image up to 10 MB, or remove it. |
| **Personal details** | Change your **First name**, **Last name** and **Job title**, then select **Save changes**. |
| **Email address** | Shown for reference. You cannot change your own email here; ask a manager. |
| **Password & access** | **Set password** emails you a reset link. |
| **Active sessions** | See and sign out your devices. See [Sessions and devices](/account/sessions-and-devices). |

Your first and last name are first set during onboarding, on the **Create your profile** step. You can change them here at any time.

## Experience

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Light, Dark or System setting. |
| **Scale** | Compact, Default (100%) or Comfortable. |

<Note>
  The Formats section (timezone, date and time format, first day of the week) saves your choice, but the console currently always shows UK date formats and weeks starting on Monday.
</Note>

## Notification preferences

Choose whether each offer event reaches you **In-app**, by **Email**, or both:

- An offer is sent to me
- An offer I sent expires unanswered
- A staff member accepts my offer
- A staff member declines my offer
- An offer I accepted is confirmed
- An offer I accepted is rejected

Other notifications, such as reminders and late clock-ins, use the default: in-app on, email off. See [Notifications](/notifications/overview).
