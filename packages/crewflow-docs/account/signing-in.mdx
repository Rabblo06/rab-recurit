This page covers the **web console**. For the mobile app, see [First sign-in](/mobile/first-sign-in).

## Who can use this

Managers, CEOs and platform administrators. Venue managers and staff sign in to the mobile app.

## How long you stay signed in

| Rule | Web console |
| --- | --- |
| Inactivity | You are signed out after **30 minutes** without activity. |
| Maximum length | A session lasts at most **24 hours**, then you sign in again. |

Closing the browser tab does not end your session. Refreshing the page keeps you signed in.

## Sign out

Open **Settings** and select **Log out** at the bottom of the settings sidebar, then confirm **Log out**. Signing out ends this session on the server, not just in your browser.

To sign out on other computers too, see [Sessions and devices](/account/sessions-and-devices).

## Common errors

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The email or password is incorrect. | The details do not match an active account. | Check for typing errors, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| This account does not have access to the Manager system. | You are a staff member or venue manager. | Use the Crewflow mobile app. |
| Too many sign-in attempts. Please try again in N minutes. | Several failed attempts in a short time. | Wait for the time shown. After 10 failures in 15 minutes the account is paused for 15 minutes. |
| Unable to reach the server. | Your connection or Crewflow is unavailable. | Check your connection and try again. |

<Note>
  For your security, Crewflow gives the same answer whether the email or the password is wrong.
</Note>
