A **role** is a named set of **permissions**. Each permission allows one kind of action, such as viewing staff or confirming offers. Crewflow works out your permissions from your role every time you make a request — they are never stored in your browser or phone.

## How a person gets their role

You choose the role when you invite someone:

| Invited as | Role given |
| --- | --- |
| **Users → New staff**, or Members → Invite as **Staff** | Staff |
| **Users → New manager** with **Internal manager**, or Members → Invite as **Manager** | Manager |
| **Users → New manager** with **Venue manager**, or Members → Invite as **Venue manager** | Venue manager |
| An organisation's first CEO (platform administrators only) | CEO |

A person's role decides which app they can use. You cannot turn a staff account into a manager account by editing it; invite the person with the role they need.

## Standard roles

| Role | Main permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **CEO** | Staff, venues, scheduling, offers, attendance, reports and the audit log, plus inviting and managing managers. |
| **Manager** | Staff, venues, scheduling, offers, attendance, reports and the audit log, plus creating venue managers in their own workspace. |
| **Venue manager** | Viewing staff, venues and schedules for assigned venues; submitting staffing requests; viewing and correcting attendance and finalising timesheets for those venues. |
| **Staff** | Responding to offers and clocking in and out. |

For the full matrix, see [Understanding user roles](/getting-started/user-roles).

## The Roles tab

**Settings → Members → Roles** lists the roles in your workspace with how many people hold each. Creating or editing a role (**+ Create role**) needs the role-management permission, which none of the standard roles include. Ask your Crewflow administrator if your organisation needs a custom role.

## Access that does not come from a role

**Platform administrator** access is granted separately by Crewflow and is independent of any organisation role. Holding it does not grant manager permissions, and holding a manager role does not grant administrator access.
