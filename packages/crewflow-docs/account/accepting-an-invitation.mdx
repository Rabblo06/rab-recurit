Nobody signs up to Crewflow on their own. Someone in your organisation creates your account and Crewflow emails you an invitation. You accept it by choosing your password.

## Who can use this

Everyone: CEOs, managers, venue managers and staff all start the same way.

## Before you begin

- Find the invitation email from Crewflow. Check your spam folder if you cannot see it.
- The link is valid for **24 hours**. If it has expired, ask the person who invited you to resend it.

## Accept your invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link in the email">
    The link opens the **Set your password** page: "Finish setting up your invited account."
    <Frame caption="The invitation link opens the Set your password page.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/activate-account.webp" alt="Set your password page with new password and confirm password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password">
    Enter it twice. It must be **10 to 128 characters** and include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a number. Common passwords and your own email address are not accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Set password">
    Crewflow shows "Your account setup is complete".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to the right app">
    - **Managers and CEOs** select **Continue to Manager Portal** and sign in to the web console.
    - **Staff and venue managers** see "You can now sign in using the ADOLPHUS mobile app" and select **Open the app**, or open the Crewflow app on their phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

| Your role | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Manager or CEO | First sign-in asks you to [create your workspace and profile](/workspaces/creating-a-workspace). |
| Venue manager | Sign in to the mobile app. You join the workspace of the manager who invited you. |
| Staff | Sign in to the mobile app and turn on fingerprint or face unlock if you like. |

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“This activation link is missing or malformed”">
    The link was cut short or changed. Copy the whole link from the email, or ask whoever invited you for a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The link has expired">
    Invitations last 24 hours. Your inviter can select **Resend invitation** on your account. Each invitation can be sent up to three times in total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I never received the email">
    Ask your inviter to check your account status in **Users**. "Delivery failed" means the email could not be delivered — check the address is correct, then resend.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
