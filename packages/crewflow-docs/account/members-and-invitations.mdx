**Settings → Members** brings your team together on three tabs: **Team**, **Invite** and **Roles**.

## Who can invite whom

| You are | You can invite |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform administrator** | An organisation's CEO and Initial Manager (from the Admin Panel), plus everyone a CEO can invite. |
| **CEO** | Internal managers, venue managers and staff. |
| **Manager** | Venue managers into your own workspace, and staff. |
| **Venue manager** | Nobody. Venue managers cannot invite members. |
| **Staff** | Nobody. |

Crewflow enforces these rules on the server. A venue manager or staff account that opens **Members** sees "Members are managed by your workspace administrator."

## Team

The **Team** tab lists the people in your workspace with their name, email, role and status. Select **Invite member** to jump to the Invite tab.

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Invite tab">
    Go to **Settings → Members → Invite**, or choose **Invite venue manager** from the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter the **Email address**, choose the **Workspace role** — **Staff**, **Venue manager**, or **Manager** (CEO and administrators only) — and enter the **First name** and **Last name**.
    <Frame caption="Invite a member by email with a workspace role.">
      <img src="/images/web/settings/members-invite.webp" alt="Members Invite tab with email, workspace role, first name and last name fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send invitation">
    Crewflow creates the account and emails the invitation. The person appears under **Pending invitations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To add a staff member with full details — phone, employment, emergency contact and availability — use **Users → New staff** instead. See [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff).
</Tip>

## How invitations behave

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid for** | 24 hours from sending. |
| **Sends** | Up to three in total, including the first. The status shows "Pending invite (1/3)" and "Final invite (3/3)". |
| **Expired** | The status becomes "Invite expired". After the final send expires, the pending account is cleaned up after 7 days. |
| **Cancelled** | **Revoke** (or **Cancel invitation**) stops the link working immediately. |
| **Delivery** | "Invitation queued", "Invitation sending…" and "Delivery failed" show the email's progress. |

From **Pending invitations**, select **Resend** or **Revoke**. The same actions are on the person's record in **Users**.
