## Who can use this

Anyone with an active Crewflow account. A manager can also send a reset link to a staff member or manager — see [Staff profiles](/staff/staff-profiles).

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a link">
    On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**. In the mobile app, tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
    <Frame caption="Request a reset link with the email address on your account.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/forgot-password.webp" alt="Forgot your password page with an email field and Send reset link button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Crewflow shows "Check your inbox". The message is the same whether or not an account exists, so nobody can use this page to discover who has an account. The link expires after **1 hour**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    The link opens **Reset your password**. Enter your new password twice and select **Reset password**. The same rules apply as when you first set it: 10–128 characters with upper-case, lower-case and a number.
    <Frame caption="Choose a new password. The page explains any rule your password does not meet.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/reset-password.webp" alt="Reset your password page with new and confirm password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a reset does

- **Signs you out everywhere.** Every web and mobile session for your account ends. Sign in again with the new password.
- **Sends a confirmation email** telling you the password changed.

## Changing your password while signed in

In **Settings → Profile → Password & access**, select **Set password**. Crewflow emails you a reset link and you follow the same steps.

## Common errors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“This link is invalid or has expired”">
    Links last one hour and work once. Request a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The email never arrives">
    Only active accounts receive reset emails. If you have not yet accepted your invitation, ask your manager to resend the invitation instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
