## Audit logging

Crewflow records sensitive actions — sign-in events, invitations, account changes, offers, attendance corrections and finalisations, workspace changes and administration — in an **append-only audit log**.

- Entries cannot be updated or deleted by anyone. The database refuses those operations even for Crewflow's own service account.
- Each entry records who acted (a person, or the system for automatic actions), what happened, and to which record.
- Managers read the log in the [Audit Log](/payroll/audit-log).

## Secure file access

Profile photos, workspace logos, shift rosters and timesheet PDFs are stored privately.

| Protection | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Access checked on every download** | Crewflow checks you may see the file before it gives you a link. |
| **Short-lived links** | Download links expire within minutes. |
| **Upload limits** | Only images can be uploaded by people (up to 10 MB). PDFs are produced by Crewflow itself. |
| **Encryption** | Cloud storage can be configured with server-side encryption at rest. |

## Personal data

- Crewflow collects what is needed to staff and pay shifts: name, contact details, emergency contact, employment details and availability.
- **Location** is used only at the moment of clocking in or out (and during a live shift while the app is open, at venues that enforce a geofence). It is not tracked in the background.
- **Biometrics** never leave the phone; Crewflow never receives fingerprint or face data.
- Pay is held as exact whole-penny amounts.
- Records with work history are kept rather than deleted, so hours and pay remain provable. Accounts are suspended or deactivated instead.

## Organisation suspension

A platform administrator can suspend an organisation. Everyone is signed out, invitations and notifications stop, and **no data is deleted**.
