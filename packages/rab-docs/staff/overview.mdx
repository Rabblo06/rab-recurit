**Users** holds everyone in your workspace in two tabs: **Staff** and **Managers**. The counts in each tab show how many people are listed.

<Frame caption="Users → Staff, with each person's reference, contact details, rate and status.">
  <img src="/images/web/staff/users-staff-list.webp" alt="Users page Staff tab listing staff with reference, email, phone, rate and status" />
</Frame>

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. Venue managers see staff for their venues in the mobile app instead.

## Columns

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | First and last name, with photo. |
| **Reference** | A staff reference such as STAFF 4, created automatically and never reused. |
| **Email** and **Phone** | Contact details. |
| **Rate** | The default hourly rate, for example £14.00/hr. |
| **Status** | The account or invitation state. See [Account states](/staff/account-states). |
| **Password** | Hidden by default. "Temporary" until the person has set their own password. |
| **Added** | When the record was created. |

## Search, filter and sort

- **Views:** All staff, Pending compliance, Active, Inactive, Suspended.
- **Filter:** Status, Employment type, Date added, Job role.
- **Sort:** Name, Staff reference, Email, Default rate, Date added.
- **Search** matches names and contact details.

## Actions on a row

Hover over a row to show its buttons: **View** the person, or — once their account is active — **Reset password**, **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**.
