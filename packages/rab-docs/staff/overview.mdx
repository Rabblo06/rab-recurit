**Users** in the sidebar holds the people in your workspace. The tabs at the top of the page show how many people each one lists, for example **Staff · 24**.

  <img src="/images/web/staff/users-staff-list.webp" alt="Users page Staff tab listing staff with reference, email, phone, rate and status" />
</Frame>

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. Which tabs you see depends on your role:

| Tab | Who sees it | Lists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Staff** | Managers and CEOs | Your staff members. |
| **Managers** | CEOs and platform administrators | Internal managers and venue managers. See [Manager accounts](/account/manager-accounts). |
| **Venue managers** | Managers who can add venue managers | The venue managers in your own workspace. See [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers). |

Venue managers do not use this page. They see the staff assigned to them in the mobile app.

## Columns

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | First and last name, with their initials. Select the name to open the profile. |
| **Reference** | A staff reference such as STAFF 4, created automatically and never reused. |
| **Email** and **Phone** | Contact details. |
| **Rate** | The default hourly rate, for example £14.00/hr. |
| **Status** | The account or invitation state. See [Account states](/staff/account-states). |
| **Password** | Hidden by default. "Temporary" until the person has set their own password, then "Active". |
| **Added** | The date the record was created. |

Use **Options** to show or hide columns.

## Search, filter and sort

- **View:** the menu at the top left switches between **All staff**, **Pending compliance**, **Active**, **Inactive** and **Suspended**.
- **Search** matches names and contact details.
- **Filter:** **Status**, **Employment type**, **Date added** and **Job role**.
- **Sort:** **Name**, **Staff reference**, **Email**, **Default rate** and **Date added** (newest or oldest first).

The footer shows how many records match. If no one matches, select **Clear filters** to see the full list again.

## Actions on a row

Hover over a row to show its buttons:

- **View** opens the person's profile.
- **Reset password** emails them a new one-time setup link. It appears once their account is active or suspended.
- **Deactivate** or **Reactivate** appears on the same accounts. See [Deactivating and deleting staff](/staff/deactivating-and-deleting).

## Select several people

Tick the box at the start of each row, or the box in the header to select everyone on the page. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen with **Selected: N** and the actions you are allowed to take: **Activate**, **Deactivate**, **Send Email** and **Delete**. If some of your selection is on another page, the bar says how many, for example "2 not on this page".

<Frame caption="Selected rows and the bulk action bar.">
  <img src="/images/web/staff/users-selection-bulk-bar.webp" alt="Staff list with three rows ticked and the bulk action bar showing Selected: 3, Activate, Deactivate, Send Email and Delete" />
</Frame>

Select the **✕** next to the bar to clear the selection. Switching tabs also clears it.
