## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs with a workspace.

## Before you begin

You need the person's **name**, **email address** and **mobile number**, plus an **emergency contact**. Everything else is optional and can be added later.

<Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
  This tutorial shows how to add a staff member. Everything you see uses fictional demonstration data.

  On the Users page, select New staff. You can also choose Add Staff from your account menu.

  Step one covers personal and employment details: the name, job role, employment type and default hourly rate.

  Step two holds the contact details. The invitation is sent to this email address.

  Step three records an emergency contact.

  Step four sets availability: working days, preferred shift times, and the maximum hours per week.

  Select Create. Crewflow creates the staff record and queues an invitation email.

  The new staff member's details open. Their status shows the invitation until they accept it and sign in to the mobile app.
</Accordion>

## Add a staff member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Users**, stay on the **Staff** tab and select **New staff** at the top right. You can also open the account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and select **Add Staff**. The **Create staff member** form has four steps and shows **Step 1 of 4** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 1 — Personal Details">
    Enter **First name** and **Last name** (required), and optionally a **Preferred name** and **Date of birth**. To add a photo, select the avatar (**Change photo**) and choose a PNG, JPEG or WEBP image up to 10 MB.

    Under **Employment**, choose a **Job role** (or **Custom role** to create one), the **Employment type** (Full-time, Part-time, Temporary, Casual or Contract), a **Start date** and the **Default rate (£/hr)**. Select **Next**.
    <Frame caption="Step 1 of 4: personal and employment details.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/create-staff-step1.webp" alt="Create staff member step 1 with name, job role, employment type and default rate" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2 — General">
    Enter the **Email** and **Mobile number** (both required), and optionally the **Address**, **City** and **Postcode**. Select **Next**.
    <Frame caption="Step 2 of 4: contact details. The email is where the invitation goes.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/create-staff-step2.webp" alt="Create staff member step 2 with email, mobile number and address fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3 — Emergency Contact">
    Enter the contact's **Full name**, **Relationship** and **Phone number** (all required). Select **Next**.
    <Frame caption="Step 3 of 4: emergency contact.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/create-staff-step3.webp" alt="Create staff member step 3 with emergency contact fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 4 — Availability">
    Optionally tick **Available days**, **Preferred shift times** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night or Flexible) and set **Maximum hours per week**.
    <Frame caption="Step 4 of 4: availability. Crewflow uses available days when it suggests replacement staff.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/create-staff-step4.webp" alt="Create staff member step 4 with available days and preferred shift times" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create">
    Crewflow creates the account and queues the invitation email. Until then you can select **Back** to change an earlier step; nothing is saved before **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

<Frame caption="After creating, Crewflow confirms the invitation was queued and opens the person's record.">
  <img src="/images/web/staff/create-staff-success.webp" alt="Confirmation that the staff account was created and an invitation queued" />
</Frame>

- The person gets a staff reference, such as **STAFF 8**.
- An invitation email is queued: "Invitation 1 of 3, expires in 24 hours". The person sets their own password — you never choose or see it.
- Their profile opens, and their status shows the invitation's progress until they activate. See [Account states](/staff/account-states).
- Once active, you can include them in offers.

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "First name is required." (or any required field) | Fill in the highlighted field. The form opens the step that needs attention. |
| "Please enter a valid email address." | Check the email for typing errors. |
| "A user with this email already exists." | That email already has a Crewflow account. Use a different email, or find the existing person. |
| "Enter the custom job role, or choose a different option." | Type the new role name, or choose an existing role. |
| "You don't have permission to create new job roles…" | Ask an administrator to add the role, then choose it from the list. |
| "Please choose a PNG, JPEG or WEBP image." or "That image is too large — the limit is 10MB." | Choose a different photo. |
| "…could not be queued. Use "Resend Invitation"…" | The account exists but the email was not queued. Open the person, select **More actions** and choose **Send invitation** or **Resend invitation**. |

<Tip>
  Staff are always added here, in **Users**. **Invite member** in the account menu (**Settings → Members → Invite**) is for people joining your workspace with a custom workspace role, not for recruitment staff.
</Tip>
