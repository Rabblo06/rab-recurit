## Send a bulk email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select recipients">
    In **Users**, tick the people you want to email. **Send Email** appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Select **Send Email**. The **To** field lists the recipients. Enter a **Subject** and **Message**.
    <Frame caption="The Send email panel.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/bulk-email-panel.webp" alt="Send email panel with To, Subject and Message fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send">
    Crewflow confirms "Queued for delivery to N recipients." If some people are outside your workspace, they are skipped and the count tells you how many.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Emails are delivered in the background and appear on each person's **Email** tab.

<Note>
  Bulk email is the only bulk action on the Users page. Selecting rows does nothing else.
</Note>
