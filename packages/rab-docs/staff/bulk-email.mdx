## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. You can email people on any tab you can see in **Users**: **Staff**, **Managers** or **Venue managers**.

## Send a bulk email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select recipients">
    In **Users**, tick the people you want to email. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen with **Selected: N**.
    <Frame caption="Selected rows and the bulk action bar.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/users-selection-bulk-bar.webp" alt="Staff list with rows ticked and the bulk action bar showing Selected: 3 and Send Email" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Select **Send Email** in the bar. The **Send email** panel opens with the number of recipients. The **To** field lists the first three names and how many more, for example "Alex Morgan, Priya Shah, Daniel Okafor +2 more". Enter a **Subject** and **Message**.
    <Frame caption="The Send email panel.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/bulk-email-panel.webp" alt="Send email panel with To, Subject and Message fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send">
    Crewflow confirms "Queued for delivery to N recipients." If some people are outside your access, they are skipped and the message says how many. Select **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Emails are delivered in the background, so recipients receive them shortly rather than instantly. Each email appears on the person's **Email** tab.

<Tip>
  To email just one person, open their profile and select **Send Email** at the bottom of the **Overview** tab.
</Tip>

<Note>
  You can send one email to up to 50 people at a time. For a bigger group, send in batches.
</Note>

The same bar also offers **Activate**, **Deactivate** and **Delete** if your role allows them. See [Deactivating and deleting staff](/staff/deactivating-and-deleting).
