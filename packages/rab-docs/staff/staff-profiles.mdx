In **Users → Staff**, select a person's name (or the **View** button on their row) to open their profile panel. The header shows their name, their staff reference (for example "Staff • STAFF 4") and their status.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs who can see the staff member in **Users**.

## Tabs

The panel shows **Overview**, **Timeline** and **Email** as tabs. Two more tabs sit under **+2 More**: **Notes** and **Staff reviews**. When you open one of them, it appears next to the other tabs and the **+2 More** button shrinks to an arrow; select the arrow to switch back.

| Tab | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Photo, name and job role, email and phone, then **Key Information**, **Personal Details** and **Additional Information** (address). Select **Show more details** for **Emergency Contact** and **Availability**. |
| **Timeline** | A history of the account, such as "Account created", "Invitation sent" and "Offer accepted". |
| **Email** | Emails sent to this person from Crewflow, with each email's delivery status. Select **+** or **Send Email** to write a new one. |
| **Notes** (under **+2 More**) | Notes from managers. Select **+ New note**, write it and select **Save note**. |
| **Staff reviews** (under **+2 More**) | Post-shift feedback from venue managers. See [Staff reviews](/staff/staff-reviews). |

## Edit details

Hover over a field and select the pencil, or select **Edit** next to the name to start with the first name.

- Text fields save when you press Enter or click away. Press Escape to cancel.
- Drop-down and date fields save as soon as you choose a value.
- For **Available days**, each day you tick or untick is saved straight away.

If a change is refused, the field shows the reason and goes back to its previous value.

**Key Information** holds the **Staff reference** (read-only), **Job role**, **Employment type**, **Start date**, **Default rate** and **Status**. Changing the default rate does not change offers already sent: each offer keeps the rate it was sent with.

### Change the photo

Select the photo and choose a PNG, JPEG or WEBP image up to 10 MB. Select **Remove photo** to go back to initials.

### Change the email address

Edit the **Email** field. If the person can already sign in, Crewflow asks you to confirm it's you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    In **Confirm it's you**, enter **Your current password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send confirmation link">
    Crewflow emails a confirmation link to the new address. The panel shows "Confirmation link sent to …. The current email stays until it is confirmed."
  </Step>
</Steps>

The address changes only when the person uses the link. For someone who has not activated yet, the new email is saved straight away.

## Employment status

Employment status records whether the person can currently be offered work:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **pending compliance** | Created and awaiting checks. |
| **active** | Can be offered work. |
| **inactive** | Not currently working. |
| **suspended** | Temporarily barred. |

Changes follow the allowed paths — for example, suspended staff can go back to active or to inactive.

## Actions

On the **Overview** tab, the bar at the bottom of the panel has **More actions** (the ⋮ button) and **Send Email**, which writes to just this person.

**More actions** offers different actions depending on the account state. Invitation actions show how many of the three allowed sends have been used, for example "2 of 3".

| Account state | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| Invite not sent yet | **Send invitation**, **Delete user** |
| Invitation pending, queued or failed | **Resend invitation**, **Cancel invitation**, **Delete user** |
| Invite expired or invitation cancelled | **Re-invite**, **Delete user** |
| Awaiting first sign-in | **Send a new setup link**, **Delete user** |
| Active or suspended | **Reset password**, **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**, **Delete user** |

Once all three invitation sends have been used, no further resend is offered.

**Reset password** asks you to confirm: "They'll be emailed a new one-time setup link and any active sessions will be signed out." **Cancel invitation** asks you to confirm that the link they were sent will stop working. For **Deactivate** and **Delete user**, see [Deactivating and deleting staff](/staff/deactivating-and-deleting).
