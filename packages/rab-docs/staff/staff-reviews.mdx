After a shift, a venue manager can review each staff member who worked it. The **Staff reviews** tab on a staff member's profile brings that feedback together.

## Who can use this

Reviews are shown in the web console to the manager who manages that staff member's records. Reviews are read-only here: you cannot edit or delete them. Staff do not see their reviews.

The tab is headed **Staff reviews** with "Post-shift feedback for" and the person's name.

## Filter the reviews

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Venue manager** (the first menu) | **All venue managers**, or one venue manager. Each name shows their venues and how many reviews they have left, or "No reviews". Type in **Search venue manager...** to find someone. |
| **Date range** | **All time**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Custom range**. A custom range shows **From** and **To** dates. |

The date range uses the date of the shift, not the date the review was submitted. Everything on the tab updates when you change a filter.

## What the tab shows

### Overall rating

The **Overall rating** card shows how many reviews there are and how many managers left them, for example "10 reviews · 2 managers". The chart in the middle shows the average score out of 5. The legend beside it shows how many reviews scored **5 stars** down to **1 star**, with the percentage for each. Hover over a part of the chart, or a legend row, to see its numbers.

For the chart, each review's score is rounded to the nearest star.

### Average by category

Each review scores five categories from 1 to 5:

- **Grooming**
- **Attitude**
- **Skills**
- **Attendance**
- **Time keeping**

The card shows the average for each, for example "4.5 / 5". When you filter to one venue manager, the heading changes to that person's average scores, for example "Sarah's average scores".

### Review history

Below the averages, **Review history** lists every review that matches your filters. When you filter to one venue manager, the heading changes to "Reviews from" and their first name. Each review shows:

- the venue manager who wrote it and the score, for example "4.2 / 5"
- the venue and the shift date
- any written feedback
- when it was submitted

## Open a single review

Select **View review** on any review in the history.

<Frame caption="A single shift review.">
  <img src="/images/web/staff/staff-review-detail.webp" alt="Shift review showing the shift, the reviewing venue manager, the review score, five category scores and written feedback" />
</Frame>

The **Shift review** shows:

- the staff member, venue, role, date and shift times
- **Reviewed by** the venue manager, and when it was submitted
- the **Review score**
- **Staff performance**: the score for each category, on the scale 1 Poor, 2 Needs improvement, 3 Satisfactory, 4 Good, 5 Excellent
- **Written feedback**, or "No written feedback was left for this shift."

Select **Back to reviews** to return to the list.

## Messages you may see

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "No reviews yet. Reviews appear here after a venue manager reviews a shift." | No one has reviewed this person yet. |
| "No reviews match these filters." | Change the venue manager or date range. |
| "Staff reviews could not be loaded." | Select **Retry**. If it keeps happening, this staff member's reviews may not be available to your account. |
| "This review is unavailable." | The review cannot be opened. Go back to the list. |
