Crewflow's background jobs watch every confirmed place. You do not need to check manually.

| Situation | When Crewflow acts | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Late** | No clock-in 10 minutes after the start | Managers are notified. The person moves to **LATE STAFF** on the live staffing board. |
| **No-show** | No clock-in 30 minutes after the start | The place is marked **no show** and managers are notified. |
| **Missing clock-out** | Still clocked in 30 minutes after the end | The attendance becomes **missing clock-out** and is flagged. |

Checks run every few minutes, so a notification may arrive a little after the exact time.

## Fixing a missing clock-out

A missing clock-out is never paid automatically. The venue manager (or a manager) corrects the clock-out time with a reason during [timesheet review](/attendance/timesheet-review). The record then moves to review.

## Breaks

Staff do not start and stop breaks in the app. Worked time deducts the **scheduled** break:

1. the break recorded on the person's attendance (for example after a correction), otherwise
2. the break set for that person when the venue manager sent the request, otherwise
3. the shift's break.

To record a different break, correct the attendance's **Break (minutes)** with a reason.

<Warning>
  Breaks are always deducted from worked time at the moment, even at venues marked as paying breaks. See [Pay calculation](/payroll/pay-calculation).
</Warning>
