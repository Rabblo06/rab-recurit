After a shift, the venue manager confirms that the recorded times are right. Finalising locks the times and produces the signed-off timesheet.

## Who can use this

Venue managers (for their venues, in the mobile app), and managers and CEOs.

## Review a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    In the mobile app, open **Reports** and tap the shift. The **Shift report** shows **Awaiting review** until it is finalised.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each person">
    Each row shows **Scheduled**, **Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Break** and **Worked**, plus the attendance status — for example **Clocked out**, **Late** or **Missing clock-out**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct anything wrong">
    Tap **Review / Edit**. Choose what to change — **Clock in**, **Clock out** or **Break (minutes)** — enter the new value and a **Reason (required)** of at least 10 characters, then **Save correction**. The row is marked **Corrected**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalise">
    When everyone has clocked out (or been marked as a no-show), tap **Finalise & send**. Until then the button reads "Waiting for everyone to clock out".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What finalising does

- Every attendance record on the shift becomes **approved**.
- The report shows "Finalised on *date, time*. The final timesheet will be emailed shortly."
- Crewflow produces the final timesheet PDF and emails it to the managers linked to the venue.
- Times can no longer be corrected.

## About signatures

<Note>
  Crewflow does not capture a drawn or typed signature. The finalised timesheet records who reviewed it and when — "Reviewed by *name* – Finalised at *time*" — while the original copy reads "Original Timesheet – Unsigned".
</Note>

## Corrections are recorded

Every correction keeps the old and new values, who made it and the reason. Corrections appear in the [audit log](/payroll/audit-log).
