After a shift, the venue manager confirms that each person's recorded hours are right. Finalising locks the hours and produces the signed-off final timesheet, which Crewflow emails to the venue's managers.

## Who can use this

- **Venue managers** review, correct and finalise timesheets in the **Crewflow app**, for shifts at their venues.
- **Managers and CEOs** follow attendance and view or download the timesheet PDFs in the web console. See [View the timesheet from the staffing board](#view-the-timesheet-from-the-staffing-board).

## The review in short

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save correction** | Changes a clock-in time, clock-out time or break, with a reason. Hours and pay are recalculated. The person then shows **Corrected · Awaiting review**. Saving a correction does **not** count as reviewing. |
| **Mark reviewed** | Confirms that this person's hours are right. The person shows **Reviewed**. Every person must be reviewed before the timesheet can be finalised. |
| **Confirm no-show** | The review action for someone recorded as a no-show. They show **No-show · Reviewed**. |
| **Finalise & send** | Opens the sign-off sheet. **Sign & send** locks the hours and starts the final timesheet. |

## Open the shift report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reports">
    In the Crewflow app, tap **Report** on Home, or go to **My Space → Reports**. Tap the shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The **Shift report** shows the **Scheduled shift** times and the number of staff, marked **To review** or **Finalised**. A notice under the summary says what is left to do, for example "2 attendance reviews remaining" or "1 clock-out time needs checking".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each person">
    Under **Staff reports**, each person's row shows **Scheduled**, **Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Break** and **Worked**, and a status badge. A missing clock-out reads **Missing**. A changed row is marked **Corrected**, and a reviewed row shows **Reviewed by** with the reviewer and time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Status badges

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Not clocked in yet. |
| **Clocked in** / **On break** | Still on shift. |
| **Needs review** | No clock-out has been recorded and the shift is over. Add the clock-out as a correction. |
| **Awaiting review** | Clocked out; the hours have not been reviewed yet. |
| **Corrected · Awaiting review** | A correction was saved; the hours have not been reviewed yet. |
| **Reviewed** | The hours were marked reviewed. |
| **No-show · Needs review** / **No-show · Reviewed** | Never clocked in; the no-show still needs confirming, or has been confirmed. |
| **Finalised** | The timesheet has been signed off. |

## Review one person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open their hours">
    Tap **Review hours** on the person's row. **Review staff report** opens on the **Attendance** tab, showing the recorded attendance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct anything wrong (optional)">
    Under **Attendance correction**, choose **Clock in**, **Clock out** or **Break**.
    - For a time, check the **Date** (change it if they clocked out after midnight) and pick the **Clock-in time** or **Clock-out time**. The screen shows "Will be saved as *date, time*".
    - For a break, enter **Break (minutes)**.

    Enter a **Reason for correction (required)** of at least 10 characters, then tap **Save correction**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved value">
    Crewflow confirms what it stored, for example "Clock-out saved: Fri 16 Oct, 22:45. Mark the attendance as reviewed when the hours are right." The row now shows **Corrected · Awaiting review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark reviewed">
    When the hours are right, tap **Mark reviewed** (or **Confirm no-show** for a no-show). You see "Attendance reviewed." and return to the report, where the person shows **Reviewed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Saving another correction after a review clears that person's review. Check the hours again and tap **Mark reviewed** again.
</Note>

### When Mark reviewed is unavailable

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Still clocked in. Review after clock-out, or once the shift has ended record the clock-out as a correction." | Wait for the person to clock out, or add the missing clock-out after the shift. |
| "Add the clock-out time as a correction first." | Add the clock-out time, then review. |
| "No attendance recorded yet. A no-show is recorded automatically 30 minutes after the start." | Wait for the no-show to be recorded, then **Confirm no-show**. |

Attendance cannot be created from the app for someone who never clocked in.

### Correction messages

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "Please explain this change in at least 10 characters." | The reason is too short. |
| "Pick a time." | No time was chosen. |
| "This is before clock in (…). If they clocked out after midnight, change the date." | The clock-out would be before the clock-in. |
| "Enter a whole number of minutes." | The break must be a whole number, 0 or more. |

A time in the future, or a change after the timesheet is finalised, is refused.

## Rate staff (optional)

The **Staff review** tab lets you rate the person from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) for **Grooming**, **Attitude**, **Skills**, **Attendance** and **Time keeping**, with optional **Feedback**, then **Save review**. All five ratings are needed to save a rating.

Ratings are **not** required to finalise the timesheet, and marking someone reviewed does not rate them. Saved ratings are printed on the final timesheet; people without one show "Not reviewed". Ratings are locked once the timesheet is finalised.

## Finalise and send

The footer of the shift report shows **Time-sheet** and, once the shift has started, **Finalise & send**.

**Finalise & send** becomes available when:

- everyone on the shift has clocked out, or has been recorded as a no-show, **and**
- every person's attendance has been marked reviewed (or their no-show confirmed).

Until then the button is greyed out and the reason is shown above it, for example "1 staff member is still clocked in", "1 clock-out time missing" or "2 attendance reviews remaining". It never appears for a cancelled shift.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-off sheet">
    Tap **Finalise & send**. **Sign off the timesheet** opens: "Confirm the report details before sending."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the breaks">
    Tick **I confirm break details are accurate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign with your name">
    Under **Manager signature**, type your full name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Sign & send**. It stays disabled until the box is ticked and your name is entered. To go back without finalising, tap **Back to report**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What finalising does

- You see "Report finalised. The final timesheet is being generated."
- Every attendance record on the shift becomes **approved**, and the hours can no longer be corrected or reviewed.
- Crewflow produces the final timesheet PDF and emails it to the managers linked to the venue. The report shows its progress: **Generating PDF**, **Queued for delivery**, then **Sent**. If the email cannot be delivered, the report shows **Delivery failed** — download the PDF from **Time-sheet** instead.
- Finalising twice has no extra effect.

## About signatures

<Note>
  Crewflow does not capture a drawn signature. The final timesheet records who finalised it and when — "Reviewed by *name* - Finalised at *time*" — and the typed sign-off: `Signed off as "name" - break details confirmed accurate`, labelled as not a handwritten signature. The original copy reads "Original Timesheet - Unsigned".
</Note>

## View the timesheet from the staffing board

Managers and CEOs can view the timesheets in the web console. They cannot correct or finalise them there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shift">
    Go to **Venue Offers** and open the shift's [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the timesheet">
    Select **Timesheet report** at the top of the page. If anyone is still clocked in you see "Timesheet is not ready yet! Wait for all staff to clock out", and nothing opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View or download">
    The **Timesheet Report** drawer lists the **Original Timesheet** and the **Venue Signed Timesheet**, each with **View** and **Download**, and a table of each person's **Clock in**, **Break**, **Clock out**, **Worked** and **Status**. Until the venue manager finalises, the signed copy reads "Waiting for Venue Manager finalisation".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Corrections are recorded

Every correction keeps the old and new values, who made it and the reason. The corrected hours and pay are what appear in [Payroll](/payroll/reviewing-payroll).
