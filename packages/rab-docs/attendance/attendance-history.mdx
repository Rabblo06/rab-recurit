| **Venue managers** | Crewflow app → **Reports** and **History** | Current shift reports, and past shifts with their timesheets. See [Reports and History](#reports-and-history-for-venue-managers). |

| **Managers and CEOs** | Web console → **Venue Offers** → a shift's staffing board → **Timesheet report** | Each person's times on that shift and the timesheet PDFs. |

## Reports and History for venue managers

Reports and History are in the Crewflow app only. Both open the same shift report, and its PDFs can still be downloaded from either.

- **Reports** — "Event staffing summaries": upcoming and current shifts, shifts waiting for clock-in or clock-out, shifts that need review or are ready to finalise, and timesheets being generated, queued or sent in the last five hours.
- **History** — "Past shifts and timesheets": shifts that ended more than five hours ago, and timesheets sent more than five hours ago.

A finalised timesheet stays in **Reports** until five hours after its email was sent, then moves to **History**. A shift that is not finalised moves to **History** five hours after it ends, but **also stays in Reports**, marked as needing attention, until it is dealt with — unfinished work is never hidden. See [Reports and timesheets](/mobile/venue-manager-reports).

## Attendance statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not clocked in | No clock-in yet. |
| Clocked in | On shift. |
| On break | On shift, on a break. |
| Clocked out | Finished; hours recorded. |
| Under review | A manager corrected the times. |
| Approved | Finalised on the timesheet. |
| Missing clock-out | Did not clock out. |
| Late | Clocked in late. |
| Absent | Did not attend. |
| Disputed | Under discussion. |

On the web staffing board's **Timesheet Report**, a corrected person's status also reads "Corrected".
