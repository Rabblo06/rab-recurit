Crewflow produces these PDFs for each shift. All are A4 landscape. They are produced automatically — nobody needs to create them.

| Document | When | Contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shift attendance sheet** | About 3 hours before the shift starts. Emailed to the managers linked to the venue as "Shift attendance sheet & QR". | The shift details, the confirmed team with each person's scheduled times and break, and the shift's **QR code** for clocking in and out. |
| **Original timesheet** | Once everyone has clocked out or been recorded as a no-show. | Each person's recorded times, break and worked hours, as recorded. Marked "Original Timesheet - Unsigned". |
| **Final timesheet** | Shortly after the venue manager signs off with **Finalise & send**. Emailed to the managers linked to the venue as "Final Timesheet". | The reviewed times, breaks and worked hours, corrections and missing clock-outs, any staff ratings ("Not reviewed" otherwise), total hours, and the manager sign-off. |

## View or download in the web console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the staffing board">
    Go to **Venue Offers** and open the shift's [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Timesheet report">
    Select **Timesheet report** at the top of the page. It opens once everyone has clocked out; before that you see "Timesheet is not ready yet! Wait for all staff to clock out".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a version">
    The **Timesheet Report** drawer shows the **Original Timesheet** ("Unsigned") and the **Venue Signed Timesheet** ("Finalised by *name* - *date, time*", or "Waiting for Venue Manager finalisation"). Select **View** to open the PDF in a new tab, or **Download** to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While a PDF is still being produced the drawer says "Generating original timesheet..." or "Generating PDF...". Your access is checked each time you open a document. If a document cannot be opened you see "Could not open this timesheet. Please retry."

The shift attendance sheet (with the QR code) is not listed in the web drawer. Use the emailed copy, or ask the venue manager.

## View or download in the Crewflow app

Venue managers open a shift report and tap **Time-sheet**:

- From about 3 hours before the shift until it is finalised, it opens the **Shift attendance sheet** — "Before shift · QR clock-in / out included".
- After finalising, it opens the **Final timesheet** — "After shift · recorded hours and feedback". While the final PDF is still being prepared, the app shows the earlier attendance sheet and says so.

Tap **Download PDF** to save the document. If it is not ready yet you see "Preparing your time-sheet"; if it cannot be loaded, "Document unavailable" with **Retry**.

## Printing

Open the PDF and print it from your PDF viewer. The shift attendance sheet is designed to be printed so the QR code can be displayed at the venue.

## Exports

<Note>
  Crewflow does not export timesheets or attendance to CSV or spreadsheets. Use the PDFs, or the [Payroll](/payroll/reviewing-payroll) page for hours and pay.
</Note>
