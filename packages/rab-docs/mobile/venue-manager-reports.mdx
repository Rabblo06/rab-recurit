## Open a report

From Home, tap **Report**, or go to **My Space → Reports**. **Reports** — "Event staffing summaries" — lists your shifts. Tap one to open its **Shift report**.

## Check each person

Each row shows **Scheduled**, **Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Break** and **Worked**, and a status such as **Clocked out**, **Late** or **Missing clock-out**.

## Correct a time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Review / Edit">
    On the person's row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to change">
    **Clock in**, **Clock out** or **Break (minutes)**, and enter the new value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    **Reason (required)** — at least 10 characters, for example "Forgot to clock out; left at 23:00 per supervisor".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Save correction">
    The row is marked **Corrected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finalise

When everyone has clocked out, tap **Finalise & send**. Until then it reads "Waiting for everyone to clock out".

After finalising: "Finalised on *date, time*. The final timesheet will be emailed shortly." Times are locked and the final timesheet PDF is emailed to the venue's managers.

<Note>
  There is no signature to draw or type. Finalising records your name and the time on the timesheet as the reviewer.
</Note>

See [Reviewing and finalising timesheets](/attendance/timesheet-review) for what finalising changes.
