## Open a report

From Home, tap **Report**, or go to **My Space → Reports**. **Reports** — "Event staffing summaries" — lists your current shifts. Tap one to open its **Shift report**.

Each card shows the shift's role, venue, date and times, and a status pill: **Upcoming**, **Waiting for clock-in**, **In progress**, **Waiting for clock-out**, **Needs review**, **Ready to finalise**, **Generating PDF**, **Queued for delivery**, **Sent**, **Delivery failed**, **Action expired** or **Cancelled**. A card may also be marked **Needs attendance review** or **Needs attention**.

With nothing current you see "No current reports." — "Sent reports move to History five hours after delivery."

## Reports and History

| List | Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reports** | Home → **Report**, or **My Space → Reports** | Upcoming and current shifts, shifts waiting for clock-in or clock-out, shifts to review or finalise, and timesheets being generated, queued or sent in the last five hours. |
| **History** | The **History** tab, or **My Space → History** | "Past shifts and timesheets": shifts that ended more than five hours ago, and timesheets sent more than five hours ago. |

**The five-hour rule:**

- A finalised timesheet stays in **Reports** until five hours after its email was sent, then moves to **History**.
- A shift that has not been finalised moves to **History** five hours after it ends, but **stays in Reports as well**, marked as needing attention, so unfinished work is never hidden. Its pill reads **Action expired**.
- Five hours after a shift ends, no new offers can be sent, accepted or confirmed for it. Reviewing and finalising its timesheet are still possible.

Every shift in either list still opens, and its PDFs can still be downloaded. With nothing in History you see "No historical activity yet."

## Check each person

The **Shift report** shows the **Scheduled shift** times and the number of staff, marked **To review** or **Finalised**, with a notice of what is left to do. Under **Staff reports**, each row shows **Scheduled**, **Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Break** and **Worked**, and a badge such as **Awaiting review**, **Corrected · Awaiting review**, **Needs review**, **Reviewed** or **No-show · Needs review**.

## Correct a time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Review hours">
    On the person's row. **Review staff report** opens on the **Attendance** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to change">
    Under **Attendance correction**, choose **Clock in**, **Clock out** or **Break**. For a time, check the **Date** and pick the time; for a break, enter **Break (minutes)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    **Reason for correction (required)** — at least 10 characters, for example "Forgot to clock out; left at 23:00 per supervisor".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Save correction">
    The app confirms the saved value, for example "Clock-out saved: Fri 16 Oct, 22:45." The row shows **Corrected · Awaiting review**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark each person reviewed

Saving a correction does not complete the review. When a person's hours are right — corrected or not — tap **Mark reviewed** on their **Attendance** tab. For a no-show, tap **Confirm no-show**. You see "Attendance reviewed." and the row shows **Reviewed**.

A later correction clears the review, so mark the person reviewed again.

## Rate staff (optional)

On the **Staff review** tab, rate **Grooming**, **Attitude**, **Skills**, **Attendance** and **Time keeping** from 1 to 5, add optional **Feedback**, and tap **Save review**. Ratings are optional and are not needed to finalise.

## Finalise and sign off

**Finalise & send** becomes available once everyone has clocked out or been recorded as a no-show, and every person has been marked reviewed. Until then it is greyed out with the reason above it, for example "2 attendance reviews remaining".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Finalise & send">
    **Sign off the timesheet** opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and sign">
    Tick **I confirm break details are accurate** and type your full name under **Manager signature**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign & send">
    You see "Report finalised. The final timesheet is being generated." Times are locked and the final timesheet PDF is emailed to the venue's managers. The report shows **Generating PDF**, **Queued for delivery**, then **Sent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There is no signature to draw. Your account, the time and the name you typed are recorded on the timesheet as the sign-off.
</Note>

## Time-sheet

Tap **Time-sheet** at the bottom of a report to open the PDF: the **Shift attendance sheet** with the QR code (from about 3 hours before the shift), or the **Final timesheet** after sign-off. Tap **Download PDF** to save it.
