## Views

Switch between **Today** and **Month** at the top. Use **Previous month** and **Next month** to move. The summary line shows "N shifts · X hours" for the period.

## What appears

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | A shift you are booked on. |
| **Open offer** | An offer waiting for your answer. |
| **Awaiting confirmation** | You accepted; waiting for a manager. |
| **Live** | You are clocked in now. |
| **Clocked Out** / **Complete** | Finished. |
| **Expired**, **Declined**, **Cancelled**, **Not confirmed** | No longer happening for you. |

Tap a shift for its details.

## Upcoming shifts

On Home, **Upcoming Shift** shows your next confirmed shifts. **view all** opens the full **Upcoming Shifts** list. "No upcoming confirmed shifts." means nothing is booked yet.

If the calendar cannot load: "Calendar unavailable — Could not load your schedule. Please try again."
