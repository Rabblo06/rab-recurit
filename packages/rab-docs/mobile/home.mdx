## organization view

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Main card** | **Live Shift**, **Today's Shift** or **Next Shift**: role, venue, date, pay rate and time. "No shift today" when there is nothing. |
| **Open Shift Offers** | How many offers are waiting for you and how many shifts are confirmed. Tap to open [Offers](/mobile/offers). |
| **Clock button** | **Clock in**, **Clock out**, **Be Ready** (shift not today), **Completed** or **No shift today**. See [Clock in and out](/mobile/clock-in-and-out). |
| **Upcoming Shift** | Your next confirmed shifts. Tap **view all** for the full list. |

The bell at the top opens your notifications, and your initials open **Profile**.

## My Space view

- **First steps** — short tips such as **Discover the app**. Tap ✕ to dismiss.
- **Overview** — quick links to **Offers**, **Confirmed** and **Upcoming shift**.

<Note>
  **My availability** and **Payslip** appear in My Space but are not available yet. Ask your manager to update your availability. Crewflow does not produce payslips.
</Note>

If offers cannot load you see "Could not load offers. Please try again." with **Retry**.
