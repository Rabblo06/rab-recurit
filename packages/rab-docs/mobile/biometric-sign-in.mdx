Biometric sign-in uses your phone's fingerprint or face recognition. The app calls it a **passkey**. Your fingerprint or face data never leaves your phone — Crewflow never sees it.

## Turn it on

- **After your first sign-in**, tap **Enable Passkey** when offered.
- **Later**, open **Profile → Security** and turn on the switch. It is labelled for your phone — **Fingerprint**, **Face Unlock**, **Face ID** or **Biometric Login**.

## Unlocking the app

Every time the app starts, it is locked. You see **Sign in with a passkey** — "Use your device biometrics. Nothing to remember." Tap **Continue with passkey** and use your fingerprint or face. Tap **Use password instead** to type your password.

If you have not turned on biometric sign-in, the app asks for your password each time it starts.

## Every 90 days

For security, you must sign in with your password at least every 90 days. When that time comes, the app shows "For your security, please sign in again."

## Problems

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Too many attempts. Use your password or try again shortly." | Tap **Use password instead**. |
| "Biometric authentication is not available on this device." | Your phone has no fingerprint or face recognition set up. Set it up in the phone's settings, or use your password. |
| "Biometrics are temporarily locked." | Your phone has locked biometrics after failed attempts. Unlock the phone with its PIN, then try again. |

<Note>
  Only fingerprint or face is accepted for passkey sign-in. Your phone's PIN cannot be used instead — use your Crewflow password.
</Note>
