## Open your offers

Tap **Open Shift Offers** on Home. **Offers** lists offers waiting for your answer and those waiting for a manager. The icon at the top right switches to your **Confirmed** shifts.

Each offer shows the role, venue, address, date and time, the hourly rate and the estimated pay — for example "£15.00/h · £82.50 estimated pay".

## Accept an offer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the offer">
    Tap it to see **Job Details**: role, venue, address, pay, date, time and any **NOTE** from your manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Accept">
    The offer now shows **Awaiting manager confirmation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Your manager must confirm you before the approval deadline (30 minutes before your start). Then the offer shows **CONFIRMED** and the shift appears in **Upcoming Shift** and your **Calendar**. If no manager confirms in time, the offer shows **Withdrawn** and you are told "Shift offer withdrawn".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decline an offer

Open the offer and tap **Decline**. You are not asked for a reason. The offer disappears from your list and your manager is told.

## Offer statuses

| You see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **PENDING** | Waiting for your answer. |
| **Awaiting manager confirmation** | You accepted; a manager has not confirmed yet. |
| **CONFIRMED** | You are on the shift. |
| **Expired** | You did not answer in time (48 hours by default). |
| **Declined** | You said no. |
| **Cancelled** | The shift was cancelled. |
| **Withdrawn** | No manager confirmed your acceptance before the approval deadline. You are not booked, and nothing counts against you. |

<Tip>
  Offers expire. Answer as soon as you can — you cannot accept an expired offer, or a standard offer less than 30 minutes before its start.
</Tip>
