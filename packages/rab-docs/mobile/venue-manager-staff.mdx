## Your team

**Users** lists the staff assigned to you. Open it from **My Space → Users**.

Your team is chosen by the manager who runs your workspace, in the Crewflow web console. You cannot add people yourself. If someone you need is missing, ask that manager to add them to your team. People they remove disappear from your list.

## Search and filter

- Use **Search staff…** to find someone by name.
- Tap **All**, **Favourite** or **Emergency** to show everyone, your favourites or your emergency cover.
- Use the filter button to filter by **Employment status**: All, Active, Pending compliance, Inactive or Suspended.

Favourite and emergency cover marks are your own. Other venue managers do not see them.

## Badges

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** / **Not Available** | Whether they can take the shift you are planning. |

Tap someone to open their **Staff Profile**. You see what you need to staff an event, never pay rates or private information. Details they have not provided show "Not provided".

<Note>
  Your **Users** team is also the list you pick from in **Select Staff** when you send a shift request.
</Note>
