## Accounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can staff sign up themselves?">
    No. A manager adds each person and Crewflow sends them an invitation. See [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't a venue manager or staff member sign in to the website?">
    They use the mobile app. The web console is for managers, CEOs and administrators.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use one account in two workspaces?">
    No. Each account belongs to one workspace, and there is no workspace switcher.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an iPhone app?">
    Not yet. The mobile app runs on Android.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't my sidebar shortcuts appear on another computer?">
    Shortcuts you add with **+** beside **OPERATIONS** are saved on this device for your account and workspace. Add them again on the other device. See [Web console overview](/getting-started/web-app-overview#add-your-own-shortcuts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I find help inside Crewflow?">
    Open **Help Center** in the **WORKSPACE** group of the sidebar for short how-to guides, or **Support** for your workspace's support contact.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Shifts and offers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do staff have to answer an offer?">
    48 hours by default. After that the offer expires. See [Offer expiry](/shifts/offer-expiry).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to confirm every acceptance?">
    For shifts you create, yes — or Crewflow confirms automatically after 60 minutes. Acceptances on shifts from approved venue requests are confirmed straight away. To see everything waiting for you, open **Offers** and choose the **Awaiting Confirmation** view.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see offers that expired?">
    Open **Offers** and choose the **Expired** view. Expired offers are no longer active and cannot be accepted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which staff can a venue manager choose?">
    Only the staff you assign to them. Open **Users**, select the **Venue managers** tab, open the venue manager and choose their staff. See [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move a shift to another day?">
    Not by dragging. Cancel the shift with a reason and create a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a pay rate change pay already agreed?">
    No. The rate is locked in when each offer is sent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Attendance and pay

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the QR code come from?">
    From the shift attendance sheet PDF, which becomes available to the venue's managers about three hours before the shift. Print it or show it at the venue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can staff clock in from home?">
    Only if the venue does not enforce its geofence. Turn on **Enforce geofence** on the venue to require staff to be on site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Crewflow pay my staff?">
    No. It calculates gross pay from attendance. Pay staff through your payroll provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are breaks paid?">
    Breaks are currently always deducted from worked time. See [Pay calculation](/payroll/pay-calculation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export timesheets to a spreadsheet?">
    Not yet. Timesheets are PDFs, and hours and pay are on the Payroll page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't the venue manager finalise the timesheet yet?">
    **Finalise & send** in the venue manager app is available only after the shift has started, everyone has clocked out, and the venue manager has reviewed each person's attendance. The app shows what is still outstanding.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
