As a venue manager you use the **Crewflow mobile app**, which opens in Venue manager mode for your account. You only see the venues you have been assigned to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation and install the app">
    Open the invitation email, set your password, then sign in to the Crewflow app on your Android phone with the same email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your team">
    Your manager chooses the staff you can see and select, from your record in the web console. You cannot add people to your team yourself. Ask your manager if someone is missing. [Staff for venue managers](/mobile/venue-manager-staff)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request staff for an event">
    Open **My Space → Sent offers** and tap **+** to open **Send Shift**. Choose the venue, role, date, times and staff, then tap **Send Shift Offer**. A manager approves the request before offers go out. [Sending a shift request](/mobile/venue-manager-send-shift)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow staffing">
    **Sent Shifts** shows how many people were asked, accepted and confirmed. The **Calendar** and **Event Details** show who is coming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and finalise the timesheet">
    After the event, open **Reports**, check each person's clock-in, clock-out and break, correct anything wrong with a reason, and mark each person's attendance as reviewed. Once everyone has clocked out and been reviewed, tap **Finalise & send**. [Reviewing timesheets](/mobile/venue-manager-reports)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Venue managers cannot use the web console. Sign in to the mobile app instead.
</Note>
