This guide is for **platform administrators**, who set up organisations, and for **CEOs**, who bring their managers in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the organisation (platform administrator)">
    In the web console, open **Settings → Admin Panel → Organisations** and select **+ New Organisation**. Enter the organisation name and the CEO's email, first name and last name. Crewflow creates the organisation and emails the CEO an invitation.
    <Frame caption="Admin Panel → Organisations lists every organisation and the status of its CEO invitation.">
      <img src="/images/web/settings/admin-panel-organisations.webp" alt="Admin Panel Organisations tab with a list of organisations" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="The CEO accepts the invitation">
    The CEO opens the email, sets a password and is taken to the web console. Invitations are valid for 24 hours and can be resent up to three times in total. See [Accepting an invitation](/account/accepting-an-invitation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite managers (CEO)">
    The CEO opens **Users**, selects the **Managers** tab and selects **New manager**, or chooses **Add manager** in the account menu (select your name at the bottom of the sidebar). Under **Manager type**, choose **Internal manager** for someone who will run their own workspace, or **Venue manager** for a client-side contact, and then **Select Venue**.
    <VideoTutorial src="/videos/managers/invite-manager.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/invite-manager.webp" captions="/videos/captions/invite-manager.en.vtt" title="Adding a manager" duration="Under 1 minute" narrated />

    <Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
      CEOs can add managers to their organisation. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

      Open Users and select the Managers tab.

      Select New manager. You can also choose Add manager from your account menu.

      Enter their first and last name.

      Keep Internal manager as the manager type, and add a job title.

      Add their work email and mobile number. The invitation is sent to this email address.

      Select Create manager. Crewflow queues the invitation and opens their details.

      Their status shows the invitation until they set a password and sign in.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Each manager creates a workspace">
    When an internal manager signs in for the first time, Crewflow asks them to create their workspace and profile. See [Creating a workspace](/workspaces/creating-a-workspace).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check email delivery (platform administrator)">
    Invitations, password resets and timesheets are sent by email using the server's configured email settings. Send yourself a test invitation or password reset and confirm it arrives, and check **Settings → Admin Panel → Health**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
