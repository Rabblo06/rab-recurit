The web console is where managers and CEOs run their workspace. Open it at [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk) and sign in with your work email.

**Who can use this:** managers, CEOs, workspace members and platform administrators. Venue managers and staff use the [mobile app](/getting-started/mobile-app-overview).

## The sidebar

From top to bottom, the sidebar has:

1. **Your workspace name.** Select it to see the current workspace and open **Workspace settings**. Each account works in one workspace, so there is nothing to switch between.
2. **Search.** Opens [search](#search). You can also press <kbd>/</kbd>.
3. **Home**, **AI** and **Settings**. These three buttons switch what the sidebar shows (see [Navigation modes](#navigation-modes)).
4. The page links for the current mode.
5. **Your name**, which opens the [account menu](#the-account-menu).

Use the button next to the workspace name to collapse the sidebar to icons, and again to expand it. On a phone-sized screen, open the sidebar with the menu button in the top bar.

### Home mode

In **Home** mode the sidebar shows three groups.

**Main pages** (at the top, no heading):

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | A live summary of offers, staff, hours and upcoming shifts. See [The Dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard). |
| **Users** | Staff and venue managers (CEOs see managers). Add people, manage invitations and edit records. |
| **Shifts** | Every shift in your workspace, with filters and actions. |
| **Payroll** | Hours and calculated pay from attendance. |
| **Offers** | Offers sent to staff. The number beside it is how many offers are currently active. |

**OPERATIONS**

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Calendar** | A month view of your shifts. |
| **Venues** | Your venues, their location settings and pay rates. |
| **Venue Offers** | Shift requests from venue managers, and the live staffing board for each shift. |

Your own shortcuts appear under these (see [Add your own shortcuts](#add-your-own-shortcuts)).

**WORKSPACE**

| Item | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Audit Log** | A record of important actions in your workspace. Shown to managers and CEOs. See [Audit log](/payroll/audit-log). |
| **Help Center** | Short how-to guides for everyday tasks, with links to the right page. |
| **Support** | How to contact support for your workspace. If no contact has been set up yet, the page says so. Ask your Crewflow administrator instead. |

Select a group heading (**OPERATIONS** or **WORKSPACE**) to collapse or expand it. Crewflow remembers this in your browser.

<Note>
  Hiding a link is never the only protection. Crewflow checks your permissions every time a page loads, so a page you cannot use shows an error or no data.
</Note>

### Navigation modes

| Mode | What the sidebar shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The workspace pages above. Returning to Home takes you back to the last page you were on. |
| **AI** | **New chat** and your **Recent drafts**. See [Ask AI](#ask-ai). |
| **Settings** | Your profile, account and workspace settings, and **Log out**. See [Profile and preferences](/account/profile-and-preferences). |

The selected mode shows its name. The others show only an icon.

## Add your own shortcuts

<VideoTutorial src="/videos/getting-started/sidebar-shortcuts.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/sidebar-shortcuts.webp" captions="/videos/captions/sidebar-shortcuts.en.vtt" title="Adding sidebar shortcuts" duration="Under 1 minute" narrated />

<Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
  You can add your own shortcuts to the sidebar. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

  Select the plus beside Operations to open Add menu item.

  A shortcut can be an Object, which is a Crewflow page, a filtered View, a Record, a Folder, or a Link.

  Choose View, and pick a filtered list.

  Give the shortcut a name and an icon, then select Add.

  The shortcut appears under Operations. Opening it goes through the normal page, so your usual access still applies.

  Use the options button on a shortcut to rename or remove it. Shortcuts are saved on this device, for your account and workspace.
</Accordion>

You can pin pages, filtered lists, records and links to the **OPERATIONS** group. Shortcuts are only links: they never change who can see something.

<Frame caption="The Add menu item popover, opened from + beside OPERATIONS.">
  <img src="/images/web/shell/add-menu-item.webp" alt="Add menu item popover listing Object, View, Record, Folder and Link" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add menu item">
    Select **+** beside **OPERATIONS**. The **Add menu item** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a type">
    - **Object**: a Crewflow page, such as **Shifts** or **Venue Offers**.
    - **View**: a filtered list, such as **Offers · Expired** or **Shifts · Upcoming**.
    - **Record**: a person, venue, shift or offer. Type at least two letters to search what you can access.
    - **Folder**: a group for your shortcuts.
    - **Link**: a Crewflow path such as `/shifts`, or a web address starting with `https://`.

    Use **Search…** to narrow the list, and **Back** to choose a different type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and add it">
    Check the **Name**, pick an **Icon** and, if you have folders, choose where to **Add to**. Select **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Result:** the shortcut appears under **OPERATIONS**. To rename or remove it, select **⋯** beside it, then **Rename** or **Remove from sidebar**. Removing a folder also removes the shortcuts inside it.

<Info>
  Shortcuts are saved on this device for your account and workspace. They do not follow you to another browser or computer, and nobody else sees them.
</Info>

**Common messages**

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That is already in your sidebar." | You already have a shortcut to that destination. |
| "Only https:// links are allowed." | Use a secure web address, or a Crewflow path starting with `/`. |
| "That is not a page in Crewflow." | Check the path, for example `/venue-offers`. |
| "You have 40 shortcuts — remove one to add another." | You have reached the limit. Remove a shortcut you no longer use. |

## Search

Press <kbd>/</kbd> (when you are not typing in a field), or select **Search** in the sidebar. Type to search across staff, managers, shifts, offers and venues. Results appear as you type, grouped by type. Select a result, or use the arrow keys and <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to open it.

## The command palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac), or select **More** (**⋯**) in the top bar. Type to filter, then select a command or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

<Frame caption="The command palette opens on the right of the screen.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/command-palette.webp" alt="Command palette with Navigation and Create commands" />
</Frame>

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Go to Dashboard**, **Go to Users**, **Go to Shifts**, **Go to Offers**, **Go to Calendar**, **Go to Payroll**, **Go to Venues**, **Go to Settings** | Opens that page. |
| **Go to Audit Log**, **View Audit Log** | Opens the Audit Log (managers and CEOs). |
| **New User** | Opens the form for adding a staff member. |
| **New Shift** | Opens Shifts and the **New shift** form. |
| **New Venue** | Opens Venues and the **New venue** form. |
| **New Manager** | Opens the form for adding a manager (CEOs). |
| **Search Users**, **Search Shifts**, **Search Offers**, **Search Venues** | Opens [search](#search). |
| **View Timeline Activities** | Opens a panel with recent workspace activity (managers and CEOs). |

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the palette.

## The account menu

Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar. The menu shows your name and email. Below that, it shows only the actions your account is allowed to take:

<Frame caption="The account menu for a manager.">
  <img src="/images/web/shell/account-menu.webp" alt="Account menu with Add Staff, Add venue manager, Invite member, Theme, Settings and Log out" />
</Frame>

| Item | Who sees it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Add Staff** | Managers and CEOs who can add staff | Opens the staff form. See [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff). |
| **Add venue manager** | Managers who can add venue managers to their workspace | Opens the venue manager form. See [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers). |
| **Add manager** | CEOs | Opens the manager form. See [Manager accounts](/account/manager-accounts). |
| **Invite member** | Managers and CEOs | Opens **Settings**, on the **Invite** tab of **Members**. See [Members and invitations](/account/members-and-invitations). |
| **Theme** | Everyone | Switches between **Light**, **Dark** and **System**. The current choice is shown beside it. |
| **Settings** | Everyone | Opens your settings. |
| **Log out** | Everyone | Asks you to confirm, then signs this device out. |

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or select outside the menu to close it.

## The top bar

- **Page title or tabs.** List pages show their tabs here. For example, **Shifts** shows **All shifts**, **Upcoming** and **Completed**, and **Offers** shows **Staff Offers** and **Venue Offers**. Pages without tabs show where you are, for example *Workspace / Venues*.
- **Page actions.** The main action for the page appears on the right, for example **New shift** or **New venue**.
- **Ask AI.** Opens the AI panel (see [Ask AI](#ask-ai)).
- **Bell.** Your notifications. See [Notifications](/notifications/overview).
- **More (⋯).** Opens the [command palette](#the-command-palette).

## Ask AI

**Ask AI** opens a panel beside the page you are on. On a smaller screen it opens the full **AI** page instead. You can type a draft, start a **New chat** and return to drafts under **Recent drafts** in the AI sidebar. Drafts are kept only while the browser tab is open.

<Note>
  No AI service is connected yet. The composer shows **AI not configured** and sending is turned off.
</Note>

## List pages

Users, Shifts, Offers, Venue Offers, Venues and Payroll share the same tools:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Tabs and views" icon="table-list">
    Tabs in the top bar and the view menu above the list switch between saved filters, such as **Upcoming** shifts or **Awaiting Confirmation** offers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search, Filter and Sort" icon="filter">
    **Search…** narrows the list. **Filter** adds conditions such as venue or status, and shows how many filters are active. **Sort** changes the order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Options" icon="sliders">
    Show or hide columns, or select **Reset columns**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Selecting rows" icon="square-check">
    On Users, tick rows (or the box in the header to select everything on the page) to act on several people at once. A bar at the bottom of the screen shows how many are selected and the actions you can take. See [Staff overview](/staff/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Paging" icon="file-lines">
    The footer shows the number of records, and the previous and next page buttons.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If a list cannot load, Crewflow says "We couldn’t load these records. Your data has not been changed." and offers **Retry**. An empty list is never shown in place of an error.
</Tip>
