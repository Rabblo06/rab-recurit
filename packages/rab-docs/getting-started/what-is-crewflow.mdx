Crewflow helps a recruitment or staffing business run its day-to-day shift work. It replaces spreadsheets, group chats and paper sign-in sheets with one system that everyone shares.

## The problem Crewflow solves

Filling shifts involves many small, time-sensitive steps: finding available people, asking them, chasing replies, confirming the team, checking everyone actually arrived and working out what each person earned. When those steps live in different places, details are lost and disputes follow.

Crewflow keeps every step on the same record:

<Steps>
  <Step title="A shift is planned">
    A manager creates the shift — venue, role, date, times, break and pay rate — or a venue manager requests one from the mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff are offered the work">
    Offers go to one or many staff members at once. Each offer shows the venue, time and estimated pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The team is confirmed">
    Staff accept in the mobile app, then a manager confirms them. Confirmation is the moment a person is placed on the shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attendance is recorded on site">
    Staff clock in and out by scanning the shift's QR code. Venues can also require staff to be within a set distance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The venue manager signs off the timesheet">
    After the shift, the venue manager reviews each person's attendance in the mobile app, then finalises the timesheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hours and pay are calculated">
    Worked time is calculated from the clock-in and clock-out times minus the break, then multiplied by the rate agreed when the offer was sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Crewflow is not

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Not a payment system" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Crewflow calculates pay from attendance. It does not transfer money, run PAYE or produce payslips.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not a public job board" icon="bullhorn">
    Staff only see offers sent to them by their own organisation. There are no public listings.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## The three apps

| App | Who uses it | Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Web console** | Managers, CEOs and platform administrators | Any modern browser |
| **Staff app** | Staff | Android (iOS is not yet available) |
| **Venue manager app** | Venue managers | The same Android app, which opens in Venue manager mode for venue manager accounts |

<Tip>
  The mobile app chooses its mode from your account. You never pick Staff or Venue manager mode yourself.
</Tip>
