<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation">
    Open the invitation email, choose a password and sign in at [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace and profile">
    Name your workspace, choose its web address, then add your first and last name. Crewflow prepares the workspace and welcomes you.
    <VideoTutorial src="/videos/managers/create-workspace.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/create-workspace.webp" captions="/videos/captions/create-workspace.en.vtt" title="Creating your workspace" duration="Under 1 minute" narrated />

    <Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
      The first time a manager signs in, they create their workspace. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

      Upload a logo, if you have one.

      Type the workspace name. The address fills in from the name.

      You can shorten the address. Crewflow checks that it is available, then select Create workspace.

      Next, create your profile so your team knows who you are, then select Complete setup.

      Crewflow prepares your workspace. It only opens once everything is genuinely ready.

      Your workspace is ready, and you land on your Dashboard.
    </Accordion>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your venues">
    Open **Venues** in the **OPERATIONS** group of the sidebar and select **New venue**. Add the address, and optionally the map location and geofence so staff must be on site to clock in. [Venues](/venues/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your staff">
    Open **Users** and select **New staff** (or choose **Add Staff** in the account menu), then complete the four steps. Each person receives an invitation email to set up the mobile app. [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add venue managers and assign their teams (optional)">
    If client-side managers will request cover, open **Users**, select the **Venue managers** tab and select **New venue manager** (or **Add venue manager** in the account menu). Assign them to a venue, then open their record and choose the staff they can see and select. [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a shift and send offers">
    Open **Shifts** and select **New shift**. Choose the venue, role, date and times, select the staff to invite, and select **Send Offer**. [Creating shifts](/shifts/creating-shifts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the people who accept">
    Open **Offers** and choose the **Awaiting Confirmation** view. Confirm people one at a time with **Confirm shift**, or all at once with **Confirm All Accepted**. [Confirming offers](/shifts/confirming-offers)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You do not have to confirm immediately. If you take no action for 60 minutes after someone accepts, Crewflow confirms them automatically and tells you.
</Tip>

## Your daily routine

| When | Where | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | **Dashboard** | Offers accepted by staff and waiting for you, open shifts, and the readiness of your next shift. See [The Dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard). |
| Before shifts | **Calendar** | Today's shifts and how many staff are allocated. |
| During shifts | **Venue Offers** (in **OPERATIONS**), then the shift | Late staff, clock-ins and clock-outs on the shift's staffing board. |
| After shifts | **Payroll** | Hours and calculated pay. |
