## Who can use this

Anyone with an active Crewflow account. A manager can also send a reset link to a staff member or manager — see [Staff profiles](/staff/staff-profiles).

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a link">
    On the sign-in page, after entering your email, select **Forgot password?**. On **Forgot your password?**, check the **Email** field and select **Send reset link**. In the mobile app, tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen.
    <Frame caption="Request a reset link with the email address on your account.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/forgot-password.webp" alt="Forgot your password page with an email field and Send reset link button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Crewflow shows **Check your inbox**: "If there’s an account for jordan.hale@example.test, we’ll send a password reset link. Check your spam folder, too." The message is the same whether or not an account exists, so nobody can use this page to discover who has an account. The link expires after **1 hour**. Select **Resend email** if it does not arrive, or **Return to sign in**.
    <Frame caption="The same confirmation appears for every address.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/forgot-password-sent.webp" alt="Check your inbox confirmation with Resend email and Return to sign in" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    The link opens **Reset your password**. Enter your **New password** and **Confirm password**, then select **Reset password**. The password must be 10 to 128 characters and include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a number. The page lists any rule your password does not meet yet.
    <Frame caption="Choose a new password.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/reset-password.webp" alt="Reset your password page with new password and confirm password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again">
    Crewflow shows **Password updated** — "You’re all set. Sign in with your new password." Managers and CEOs select **Continue to Manager Portal**. Staff and venue managers are told to sign in to the mobile app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a reset does

- **Signs you out everywhere.** Every web and mobile session for your account ends. Sign in again with the new password.
- **Sends a confirmation email** telling you the password changed.

## Changing your password while signed in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to **Settings → Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Set password">
    In **Password & access**, select **Set password**. Crewflow shows "Check your email for a link to set a new password."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the link">
    Open the email and follow the same steps as above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common errors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“This link is invalid or has expired”">
    Links last one hour and work once. Request a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“This link has expired”">
    The link was cut short or opened without its code. Select **Request a new link**, or copy the whole link from the email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“Passwords don't match.”">
    The two password fields are different. Type them again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The email never arrives">
    Check your spam folder and select **Resend email**. Only active accounts receive reset emails. If you have not yet accepted your invitation, ask your manager to resend the invitation instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
