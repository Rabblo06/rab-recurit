Your personal settings live under **Settings → Your account**, on three pages: **Profile**, **Experience** and **Account**. Open **Settings** from the mode switcher at the top of the sidebar, or from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Who can use this

Everyone who signs in to the web console. These settings only affect your own account.

## Profile

<Frame caption="Settings → Profile.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/settings-profile.webp" alt="Settings Profile page with profile photo, personal details, email address, password and access, and devices" />
</Frame>

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile photo** | Select **Upload photo** to add a PNG, JPG or WEBP image of up to 10 MB, or **Remove** to delete it. |
| **Personal details** | Change your **First name**, **Last name** and **Job title**, then select **Save changes**. |
| **Email address** | Shown for reference. You cannot change your own email here; ask a manager. |
| **Password & access** | **Set password** emails you a secure link to set a new password. |
| **Devices** | See where you are signed in and log out other devices. See [Sessions and devices](/account/sessions-and-devices). |

### Edit your details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change a field">
    Edit **First name**, **Last name** or **Job title**. **Save changes** becomes available as soon as something differs from your saved details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save changes">
    Crewflow shows "Profile updated." Your new name appears in the sidebar and to your team straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you leave the page without saving, nothing is changed. Your first and last name are first set during onboarding, on the **Create your profile** step.

## Experience

<Frame caption="Settings → Experience.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/settings-experience.webp" alt="Settings Experience page with Appearance, Interface, Navigation and Formats sections" />
</Frame>

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | **Light**, **Dark** or **System setting**. |
| **Interface** | **Language** (English) and **Scale**: **Compact**, **Default — 100%** or **Comfortable**. |
| **Navigation** | Where supported records and forms open: **Side panel — open alongside the current page** or **Full page — open records on a dedicated page**. |
| **Formats** | **Timezone**, **Date format**, **Time format** and **First day of week**. |

Appearance and Scale apply straight away and are remembered by this browser, so another computer can use a different setting. Navigation and Formats choices are saved to your account and Crewflow shows "Preference saved."

<Note>
  The Formats section saves your choice, but the console currently always shows UK date formats and weeks starting on Monday.
</Note>

You can also switch the theme from the account menu: select **Theme** to cycle through the options.

## Account

<Frame caption="Settings → Account.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/settings-account.webp" alt="Settings Account page with Account Authentication, Emails and Notifications sections" />
</Frame>

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Account Authentication** | How you sign in. |
| **Emails** | Who manages email delivery. Platform administrators see **Configure in Admin Panel**; everyone else sees "Managed by your workspace administrator". |
| **Notifications** | Your offer notification preferences. |

### Notification preferences

Choose whether each offer event reaches you **In-app**, by **Email**, or both. Each tick saves straight away.

- An offer is sent to me
- An offer I sent expires unanswered
- A staff member accepts my offer
- A staff member declines my offer
- An offer I accepted is confirmed
- An offer I accepted is rejected

Other notifications, such as reminders and late clock-ins, use the default: in-app on, email off. See [Notifications](/notifications/overview).

## Common errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Could not save your profile. Please try again. | Check your connection and select **Save changes** again. |
| Could not upload that file. | Use a PNG, JPG or WEBP image of 10 MB or less. |
| Could not save that preference. | Try again. Your previous choice is kept. |
