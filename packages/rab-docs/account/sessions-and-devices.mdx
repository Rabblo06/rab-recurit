Each time you sign in on a browser or phone, Crewflow starts a **session**. You can see and end your sessions from the web console.

## View your sessions

Open **Settings → Profile** and scroll to **Active sessions**. Each row shows the browser or device, when it was last active, and the network address. The device you are using now is marked **This device**.

## Sign out other devices

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Revoke** on a row | Ends that one session. |
| **Sign out of all other devices** | Ends every session except this one. |
| **Sign out everywhere** | Ends every session, including this one. |

Each bulk action asks you to confirm first. Ended sessions stop working on their next request; they cannot be reused.

## How long sessions last

| App | Signed out after inactivity | Maximum length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web console | 30 minutes | 24 hours |
| Mobile app (staff and venue managers) | 7 days | 90 days |

Managers and CEOs who sign in to the mobile app keep the stricter web limits.

## When Crewflow ends sessions for you

- **Password reset or change** — every session ends.
- **Account deactivated or suspended** — the person is signed out.
- **Organisation suspended** — everyone in the organisation is signed out until it is reactivated.

<Tip>
  If you see a device you do not recognise, select **Sign out everywhere** and then [reset your password](/account/password-reset).
</Tip>
