Each time you sign in on a browser or phone, Crewflow starts a **session**. You can see your sessions, and end the ones on other devices, from the web console.

## Who can use this

Anyone who signs in to the web console: managers, CEOs, workspace members and platform administrators. Each person only ever sees their own sessions.

## View your devices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Open **Settings** and select **Profile** under **Your account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Devices card">
    The **Devices** card lists "Devices with an active session on your account." The device you are using now is listed first and marked **This device**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Settings → Profile → Devices.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/settings-profile.webp" alt="Devices card listing Chrome on Windows marked This device, two other devices with a more options button, and Log out all other devices" />
</Frame>

Each row shows:

| Detail | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Browser and system** | Chrome on Windows, Safari on macOS, Mobile app (Dart client) |
| **Last activity** | Last active 2 minutes ago |
| **Network address** | 203.0.113.24, or **Private network (…)** when the address belongs to a private or local network |

Sign-ins made by automated tools are labelled as such, for example **Automated browser** or **Script or API client**, so they are not mistaken for a person's browser. A label describes the software that signed in, not the physical device.

## Log out one device

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row's menu">
    On the device you want to remove, select **…** (**More options**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Log out">
    Crewflow ends that session straight away and shows "Device logged out."
  </Step>
</Steps>

The device you are using now has no menu. To end this session, use **Log out** in the Settings sidebar or the account menu — see [Signing in and out](/account/signing-in#sign-out).

## Log out all other devices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Log out all other devices">
    The button appears under the list when at least one other device is signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog **Log out all other devices?** explains: "This device stays signed in. Every other session is ended immediately." Select **Log out other devices**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Crewflow shows "Logged out all other devices." and the list keeps only **This device**. A logged-out device is refused on its next request and has to sign in again.

## How long sessions last

| Who | Signed out after inactivity | Maximum length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Managers, CEOs and workspace members (web console) | 30 days | 180 days |
| Platform administrators | 30 minutes | 24 hours |
| Staff and venue managers (mobile app) | 7 days | 90 days |

Managers and CEOs who sign in to the mobile app follow the manager limits above.

## When Crewflow ends sessions for you

- **Password reset or change** — every session ends, on the web and on mobile.
- **Account deactivated or suspended** — the person is signed out on every device.
- **Organisation suspended** — everyone in the organisation is signed out until it is reactivated.

<Tip>
  If you see a device you do not recognise, select **Log out all other devices** and then [reset your password](/account/password-reset). A reset also ends every session, including this one.
</Tip>

## Common problems

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your devices could not be loaded. Check your connection. | Check your connection and select **Retry**. |
| The session service is unavailable right now, so your devices could not be loaded. | Wait a moment and select **Retry**. |
| Your session has ended, so your devices could not be loaded. Sign in again. | Sign in again. |
| Failed to log out this device. | Try again. If it keeps failing, reload the page. |
