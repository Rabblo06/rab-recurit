A **role** is a named set of **permissions**. Each permission allows one kind of action, such as viewing staff or confirming offers. Crewflow works out your permissions from your role every time you make a request — they are never stored in your browser or phone.

## How a person gets their role

The way you add someone decides their role:

| Who | How you add them | Role given |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff | **Users → New staff**, or **Add Staff** in the account menu | Staff |
| Venue managers | **Users → Venue managers → New venue manager**, or **Add venue manager** in the account menu | Venue manager |
| Managers | **Users → Managers → New manager**, or **Add manager** in the account menu (CEOs and platform administrators) | Manager |
| Workspace members | **Settings → Members → Invite** (or **Invite member** in the account menu), choosing one of your custom workspace roles | The custom role you choose |
| An organisation's first CEO | Created by a platform administrator | CEO |

A person's role decides which app they can use. You cannot turn a staff account into a manager account by editing it; add the person in the way that matches the role they need. Staff, venue managers and managers are never invited from **Members** — that tab only offers your custom workspace roles.

## Standard roles

| Role | Main permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **CEO** | Staff, venues, scheduling, offers, attendance, reports and the audit log, plus adding and managing managers. |
| **Manager** | Staff, venues, scheduling, offers, attendance, reports and the audit log, plus adding venue managers in their own workspace and choosing which staff each one can see. |
| **Venue manager** | Viewing their assigned staff and their venues' schedules; sending staffing requests; reviewing and correcting attendance and finalising timesheets for their venues in the mobile app. |
| **Staff** | Responding to offers and clocking in and out. |

For the full matrix, see [Understanding user roles](/getting-started/user-roles).

## The Roles tab

**Settings → Members → Roles** shows **Workspace roles**. Each card shows the role's name, a short description and how many members have it:

- **System role · read-only.** — the standard roles. They apply to the whole organisation and can't be edited here.
- **Custom workspace role — invite people with it.** — a role you created that can be given to workspace members.
- **Custom workspace role · not assignable.** — a custom role that is switched off for new members.

Use **Search a role…** to find a role, and **+ Create role** to make a new one.

## Create a workspace role

A custom role is for people who help run your workspace without being managers. It belongs to your workspace only and can never grant more than you hold yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new role">
    Open **Settings → Members → Roles** and select **+ Create role**. The role editor opens with two tabs: **Permissions** and **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the role">
    On **Settings**, choose an icon, enter a name (it starts as *Untitled role*) and an optional description. Tick **Assignable to Workspace Members** so you can invite people with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it can see">
    On **Permissions**, under **Objects**, **See Records on All Objects** gives read access to every object at once. To set access object by object, select **Add rule**, pick an object in **1. Select an object**, then in **2. Set permissions** choose whether the role can see that object (or **Follow All Objects**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit fields and records (optional)">
    In the rule, **Fields Permissions** lists each field with **See** and **Edit** columns; use **See all fields** or untick individual fields. Fields marked **Sensitive** hold personal details. **Record-level** filters narrow which records are visible: choose whether records must match **all of** or **any of** the conditions, then use **Add condition** or **Add group**. Select **Finish** to keep the rule, or **Remove rule** to delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. You see *Role created.* (or *Role saved.* when you edit an existing role).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Editing, deleting and other write access are shown in the editor but can't be granted to custom roles: workspace members can view information only.

If you leave the editor with unsaved changes, Crewflow asks *Discard your unsaved changes to this role?* first.

### Delete a custom role

Open the role, go to **Settings** and select **Delete role**, then confirm. You can only delete a role that no member has and that no pending invitation uses.

## Access that does not come from a role

**Platform administrator** access is granted separately by Crewflow and is independent of any organisation role. Holding it does not grant manager permissions, and holding a manager role does not grant administrator access.
