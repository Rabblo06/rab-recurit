**Settings → Members** brings your workspace's people together on three tabs: **Team**, **Invite** and **Roles**.

Members are the people you invite into **your own workspace** with a custom workspace role, plus you as its owner. Recruitment **staff** and **venue managers** are not members in this sense. You add and manage them in **Users** (see [Adding staff](/staff/adding-staff) and [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers)), and they never appear in the Members role lists.

## Who can do what

| You are | Invite | Add (leadership) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Platform administrator** | — | Create an organisation and its CEO invitation; invite or manage an organisation's Initial Manager. |
| **CEO** | Invite people into your own workspace with one of your workspace roles. | Invite your organisation's Initial Manager. |
| **Manager** (including the Initial Manager) | Invite people into your own workspace with one of your workspace roles. | — |
| **Venue manager, staff, workspace member** | — | — |

Crewflow enforces every rule on the server, not only in the page. Someone without access who opens **Members** sees "Members are managed by your workspace administrator."

## Roles

The **Roles** tab lists two kinds of role:

- **System roles** (Manager, CEO, Venue Manager, Staff) apply to the whole organisation. They are shown for reference and can't be changed here.
- **Custom workspace roles** belong to your workspace only. Other managers can't see, edit or use them.

Select **+ Create role**, name it, and choose what people with this role can see. Workspace roles can **view** information only:

| Permission | What members can see |
| --- | --- |
| View staff | The staff **you** added, in Users. |
| View venues | **Your** venues. |
| View shifts | **Your** shifts and job roles. |

You can only give a permission you hold yourself. Open a role to see the members of your workspace who have it.

## Invite people by email

You only need email addresses and a workspace role. Each person enters their own first name, last name and password when they accept, then joins **your** workspace. Inviting someone never creates a new workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace role first">
    If you have no custom role yet, the Invite tab says so and links to **Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the addresses and choose a role">
    Go to **Settings → Members → Invite**. Type one or more addresses in **Email address**, separated by commas (up to 20 at a time), and choose the **Workspace role**. The line under the fields explains what the role can see. CEOs and platform administrators also see a small **Add | Invite** selector at the start of the email field. Leave it on **Invite**.
    <Frame caption="Invite people by email with one of your workspace roles. There are no name fields.">
      <img src="/images/web/settings/members-invite.webp" alt="Members Invite tab with an email address field, a workspace role list and a Send invitation button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send invitation">
    Crewflow shows a result for every address. Addresses that could not be invited stay in the field so you can correct them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Invitation queued** | The invitation was created and its email is on its way. |
| **Already has a pending invitation** | A live invitation already exists for this address. Resend that one instead. |
| **Already has an account in this organisation** | This person already has an account, for example as staff. |
| **Not a valid email address** | Check the spelling. |
| **Can’t be invited right now** | The address can’t be invited from your workspace. Crewflow doesn’t say why, so it never reveals details about another workspace. |

Invited members sign in to the **web console** only. If you suspend their workspace membership, they can't sign in at all.

## Pending invitations

Below the form, **Pending invitations** lists every invitation that hasn't been accepted yet, with its **Role**, delivery **Status** and when it **Expires in**. Use the status filter to show only queued, sent, failed or expired invitations.

<Frame caption="Pending invitations, with Resend and Revoke.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/members-pending-invitations.webp" alt="Invite tab with a pending invitation showing role, status, expiry and Resend and Revoke buttons" />
</Frame>

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid for** | 24 hours from the latest send. |
| **Sends** | Up to three in total, including the first. **Resend** issues a new link and the previous link stops working at once. After the third send, revoke the invitation and invite the person again. |
| **Status** | **Queued**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Delivery failed** or **Expired**, taken from the real email delivery record. |
| **Revoke** | Asks you to confirm, then stops the link working immediately. |
| **Single use** | A link works once. After it is accepted, revoked, replaced by a resend or expired, it shows "This invitation can’t be used". |
| **Checked again on acceptance** | If you no longer have the role permissions, or the role now grants something you don't hold, the invitation can't be accepted. |

## Team

The **Team** tab lists the members of your workspace: you as its owner, and everyone who accepted one of your invitations. It shows each person's name (or their email until they've set one), email, role and status. Search and paging happen on the server. Only the workspace owner can see this list.

## Add organisation leadership

CEOs and platform administrators can set the selector at the start of the email field to **Add**. It holds the leadership actions only:

- **Platform administrators:** **Create organisation** (the organisation and its CEO invitation), or pick an organisation to manage its CEO and Initial Manager.
- **CEOs:** **Invite Initial Manager** for your own organisation.

These leadership invitations still ask for the person's first and last name. Managers, including the Initial Manager, never see **Add**.
