This page covers the **web console**. For the mobile app, see [First sign-in](/mobile/first-sign-in).

## Who can use this

Managers, CEOs, workspace members and platform administrators. Venue managers and staff sign in to the mobile app.

You arrive on your Dashboard. If your account needs a new password first, Crewflow takes you to **Set your password** before anything else. A manager or CEO signing in for the first time is asked to [create a workspace and profile](/workspaces/creating-a-workspace).

## How long you stay signed in

| Who | Signed out after inactivity | Maximum length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Managers, CEOs and workspace members | 30 days | 180 days |
| Platform administrators | 30 minutes | 24 hours |

When the limit is reached you are asked to sign in again. Closing the browser tab does not end your session, and refreshing the page keeps you signed in. On a shared computer, always log out when you finish.

## Sign out

You can log out from either place:

- **Settings** → **Log out**, in the **Other** section at the bottom of the settings sidebar.
- The account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) → **Log out**.

Crewflow asks **Log out?** — "Are you sure you want to log out of your account?" Select **Log out** to confirm. Logging out ends this session on the server, not just in your browser, and any other tabs in the same browser are logged out too.

To log out other computers or phones, see [Sessions and devices](/account/sessions-and-devices).

## Common errors

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The email or password is incorrect. Check your details and try again. | The details do not match an active account. | Check for typing errors, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| This account does not have access to the Manager system. | You are a staff member or venue manager. | Use the Crewflow mobile app. |
| Too many sign-in attempts. Please try again in N minutes. | Several failed attempts in a short time. | Wait for the time shown; the button reads **Please wait before trying again** until then. After 10 failures in 15 minutes the account is paused for 15 minutes. |
| Unable to reach the server. Check your connection and try again. | Your connection or Crewflow is unavailable. | Check your connection and select **Try again**. |
| Unable to sign in right now. Please try again. | Crewflow could not complete the sign-in. | Wait a moment and try again. |
| Sign-in is not available. Please contact your workspace administrator. | No sign-in method is currently switched on. | Contact your administrator. |

<Note>
  For your security, Crewflow gives the same answer whether the email or the password is wrong.
</Note>
