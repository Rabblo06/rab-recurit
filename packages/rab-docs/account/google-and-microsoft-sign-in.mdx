Crewflow supports signing in with Google or Microsoft as an alternative to a password. The buttons only appear when the provider is switched on for the Crewflow service you use, and your organisation must also allow it.

## Who can use this

Managers, CEOs and workspace members who sign in to the web console, when Google or Microsoft sign-in is available.

## How it works

- **Invitation first.** Google and Microsoft sign-in never create a Crewflow account on their own. You must already have been invited, and the email on your Google or Microsoft account must match your Crewflow email.
- **Verified emails only.** For Google, the email must be verified by Google.
- **Organisation rules apply.** If your organisation has not allowed a provider, signing in with it is refused even though the button is shown.
- **Same session rules.** You stay signed in under the same limits as password sign-in — see [Signing in and out](/account/signing-in#how-long-you-stay-signed-in).

## Sign in with Google or Microsoft

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [{{appUrl}}](https://app.rabworkspaceteams.co.uk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the provider">
    Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** and follow the provider's screens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Crewflow">
    Crewflow shows "Signing you in…" and then opens the page you were going to. If your account still needs a password set, Crewflow asks you to set one first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accept an invitation with Google or Microsoft

If you received an email invitation to join a workspace, the invitation page can offer **Accept with Google** or **Accept with Microsoft**. See [Accepting an invitation](/account/accepting-an-invitation#accept-with-google-or-microsoft).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Try signing in again** — "Something didn’t go as planned. Let’s give it another try." | Sign-in did not complete. | Check that the provider account's email matches your Crewflow email and that your organisation allows the provider, then try again. Cancelling at the provider's screen also brings you here. |
| **We couldn’t reach Crewflow** | Your connection dropped while finishing sign-in. | Select **Try again**. You haven’t been signed out. |
| **Additional verification required** | Your workspace needs a verification step that is not available for this sign-in method. | Select **Back to sign in** and contact your administrator. |

<Note>
  The mobile app uses email and password, plus fingerprint or face unlock. Google and Microsoft sign-in are web console features.
</Note>
