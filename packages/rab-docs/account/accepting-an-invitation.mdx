Nobody signs up to Crewflow on their own. Someone in your organisation invites you and Crewflow emails you a link. How you accept depends on how you were invited:

- **Invited into a workspace from Settings → Members** (workspace members with a custom role): you enter your own name and choose your password on the **Join** page, then use the web console.
- **Added with full details** (staff and venue managers from **Users**, managers, or a CEO or Initial Manager invitation): your name is already filled in, and you only choose a password on the **Set your password** page.

## Who can use this

Everyone: CEOs, managers, venue managers and staff all start with an invitation.

## Before you begin

- Find the invitation email from Crewflow. Check your spam folder if you cannot see it.
- The link is valid for **24 hours** and works **once**. If it has expired, ask the person who invited you to resend it.

## Accept an email invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link in the email">
    The **Join** page shows the workspace and the role you were invited as. Your email address is filled in and can’t be changed.
    <Frame caption="The invitation link opens the Join page.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/accept-invitation.webp" alt="Join ADOLPHUS QA Workspace page with a read-only email and first name, last name, password and confirm password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your name and choose a password">
    Enter your **First name** and **Last name**, then your password twice. It must be **10 to 128 characters** and include an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a number. Common passwords and your own email address are not accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Accept invitation">
    Crewflow shows "Your account setup is complete".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to the right app">
    Workspace members sign in to the **web console**. What you can see there depends on your role. The mobile apps are for staff and venue managers only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accept a full-details invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link in the email">
    The link opens the **Set your password** page: "Finish setting up your invited account."
    <Frame caption="A full-details invitation opens the Set your password page.">
      <img src="/images/web/auth/activate-account.webp" alt="Set your password page with new password and confirm password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a password and select Set password">
    The same password rules apply. Then continue to the web console or the mobile app as above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

| Your role | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace member | Sign in to the web console. Your first sign-in finishes activating your account. You join the inviter's workspace and see what your role allows. |
| Manager or CEO | Your first sign-in finishes activating your account and asks you to [create your workspace and profile](/workspaces/creating-a-workspace). |
| Venue manager | Sign in to the mobile app. You join the workspace you were invited to. |
| Staff | Sign in to the mobile app and turn on fingerprint or face unlock if you like. |

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“This invitation can’t be used”">
    The link has expired, was already used, was revoked, or was replaced by a newer email. Crewflow shows the same message for all of these. Use the most recent invitation email, or ask whoever invited you to send a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“We couldn’t check your invitation”">
    Crewflow is limiting repeated attempts or is briefly unavailable. Your link has not been used. Wait a minute and select **Try again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“This activation link is missing or malformed”">
    The full-details link was cut short or changed. Copy the whole link from the email, or ask for a new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I never received the email">
    Ask your inviter to check **Settings → Members → Invite → Pending invitations**. "Delivery failed" means the email could not be delivered. Check the address, then resend.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
