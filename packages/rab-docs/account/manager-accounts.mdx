CEOs and platform administrators see manager accounts in **Users → Managers**. There are two kinds:

| Type | Uses | Works in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Internal manager** | The web console | Their own workspace, which they create at first sign-in |
| **Venue manager** | The mobile app | The workspace of the manager who invited them, limited to assigned venues |

## Who can use this

CEOs and platform administrators see the **Managers** tab, **New manager**, and **Add manager** in the account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar).

Managers see a **Venue managers** tab instead, for the venue managers in their own workspace. They add them with **New venue manager** or **Add venue manager** in the account menu. See [Venue managers](/venues/venue-managers).

## The Managers tab

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Select it to open the manager's details. |
| **Email**, **Phone** and **Job title** | Contact and role details. |
| **Type** | **Manager** or **Venue manager**. |
| **Status** | The account or invitation state. See [Account states](/staff/account-states). |
| **Password** | Hidden by default. "Temporary" until they set their own password. |
| **Added** | How long ago the account was created. |

Use the view menu (**All managers**, **Invited**, **Active**, **Suspended**, **Deactivated**, **Invite expired**), **Search**, **Filter** (**Status**, **Type**, **Date added**) and **Sort** (**Name**, **Email**, **Job title**, **Date added**) to find someone.

## Manage a manager's account

Select the manager's name, or the **View** button on their row, to open their details. The header shows "Manager" and their type, and **Key Information** shows the **Manager type**: **Internal manager**, **Venue manager**, **CEO** or **Initial Manager**. The **Timeline**, **Email** and **Notes** tabs work as they do for staff (**Notes** is under **+1 More**).

From **More actions** (⋮) at the bottom of the **Overview** tab you can:

- **Send invitation**, **Resend invitation**, **Re-invite** or **Cancel invitation** while they have not activated, depending on the invitation's state
- **Send a new setup link** if they set a password but have not signed in yet
- **Reset password**, which emails them a one-time setup link
- **Suspend** or **Reactivate** the account — suspending signs them out everywhere
- **Delete user**, which requires typing DELETE and your current password

You can also hover over a row for **Reset password** and **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**, or tick several rows and use **Activate**, **Deactivate**, **Send Email** or **Delete** in the bar at the bottom of the screen. See [Deactivating and deleting staff](/staff/deactivating-and-deleting) and [Bulk email](/staff/bulk-email).

<Warning>
  Accounts with historical shifts or attendance cannot be deleted permanently: "This user has historical workforce records and cannot be permanently deleted. Suspend the account instead."
</Warning>

## The Initial Manager

A CEO or platform administrator can name one **Initial Manager** per organisation — the first internal manager. The Initial Manager receives a dedicated invitation and sets up their own workspace at first sign-in. Platform administrators manage this from **Admin Panel → Organisations** (see [Organisation administration](/account/organisation-administration)).
