The **Payroll** page lists attendance with the hours worked and the pay calculated for them. Use it to check figures before you pay staff through your usual payroll provider.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. You see attendance for the staff you manage.

## Open Payroll

Select **Payroll** in the sidebar. Each row is one person's attendance on one shift. **All records** (shown at the top of the page) lists everything, newest first.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff** | Who worked. |
| **Venue** | Where. |
| **Shift date** | When. |
| **Hours** | Worked hours, for example 5.60h. Shows "–" until the person has clocked out. |
| **Amount** | Calculated pay, for example £84.00. Shows "–" until the person has clocked out. |

Use **Options** (**…**) to show or hide columns. **Staff** and **Amount** are always shown.

## Narrow it down

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search...** to find a staff member's first or last name, or a venue name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Select **Filter** to add conditions: **Venue**, **Date** (a date range), **Hours (minutes)** or **Amount (pence)**. The number on the button shows how many filters are active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    Select **Sort** and choose **Date** (**Newest first** or **Oldest first**), **Staff**, **Venue**, **Hours** or **Amount**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Filters, search and sort are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark a view or open it in a new tab with the arrow button next to **Options**.

If nothing matches you see "No results match these filters." — select **Clear filters** to start again.

## Read the totals

The footer shows how many records match, for example "25 records", and the total hours and amount for the rows shown, for example "140.72h · £2028.52". Filtering by venue and date range gives that venue's total for the period. Select **Calculate** to show the number of rows on the page.

Up to 500 records are shown per page. If there are more, use the arrows in the footer to move between pages; the totals cover the rows on the current page.

## Selecting rows

Ticking rows shows a bar at the bottom of the screen with **Selected:** and the count. There are no bulk actions for payroll; select **×** to clear the selection.

## Checking a figure

If hours look wrong, open the shift's timesheet from its [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline) and ask the venue manager to correct the attendance before finalising. Corrections update the hours and amount here.

<Note>
  There are no approve, export or mark-as-paid buttons. Copy figures into your payroll provider manually, or use the [timesheet PDFs](/attendance/timesheet-documents).
</Note>

## If the page does not load

You see "We couldn’t load these records." with **Retry**. Your data has not been changed. Select **Retry**, and if it keeps happening, contact your administrator.
