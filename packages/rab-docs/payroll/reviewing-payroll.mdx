## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Open Payroll

Select **Payroll** in the sidebar. Each row is one person's attendance on one shift.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff** | Who worked. |
| **Venue** | Where. |
| **Shift date** | When. |
| **Hours** | Worked hours, for example 5.60h. |
| **Amount** | Calculated pay, for example £84.00. |
| **Status** | **Completed** (clocked out) or **In progress** (still on shift). |

The footer totals the hours and amount for the rows shown.

## Narrow it down

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    **All records**, **In progress** or **Completed**. Use **Completed** for finished work.
    <Frame caption="The Completed tab shows only finished attendance.">
      <img src="/images/web/payroll/payroll-completed-tab.webp" alt="Payroll Completed tab with completed attendance and totals" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Select **Filter** to add conditions: **Venue**, **Status**, **Date**, **Hours (minutes)** or **Amount (pence)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    By Date, Staff, Venue, Hours or Amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The footer totals update to match your filters, so filtering by venue and date range gives that venue's total for the period.

## Checking a figure

If hours look wrong, check the shift's timesheet on the [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline) and ask the venue manager to correct the attendance before finalising. Corrections update the hours and amount here.

<Note>
  There are no approve, export or mark-as-paid buttons. Ticking rows only highlights them. Copy figures into your payroll provider manually, or use the timesheet PDFs.
</Note>
