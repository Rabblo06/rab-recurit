The **Audit Log** lists the sensitive actions **you** have performed, as they happen — for example offers you sent or confirmed, people you invited, and roles or settings you changed. Entries can never be edited or deleted.

<Note>
  The Audit Log shows your own actions only. It does not list actions performed by other people or automatically by Crewflow.
</Note>

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Open the Audit Log

Select **Audit Log** in the sidebar, under **Workspace**. You can also open the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) and choose **Go to Audit Log**.

<Frame caption="The Audit Log, newest first.">
  <img src="/images/web/settings/audit-log.webp" alt="Audit Log listing when, performed by, action, category and record" />
</Frame>

The top of the page shows the number of entries, for example "128 entries".

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | How long ago, for example "5 minutes ago". Point at it to see the exact date and time. |
| **Performed by** | Who performed the action — you. |
| **Action** | What happened, for example *offer confirmed* or *user invited*. |
| **Category** | The kind of record, for example offer, user or role. |
| **Record** | The record affected, by name where one is available. |

Use **Options** (**…**) to show or hide **Performed by**, **Category** and **Record**.

## Find an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Select **Filter** and choose an **Action** (for example **Offer Confirmed**), a **Category** (attendance, manager, manager workspace, offer, organisation, role or user) or a **Date** range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    Select **Sort** and choose **Newest first** or **Oldest first**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in **Search...** to narrow the entries on the current page by action, name or category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The log shows 50 entries per page. Use the arrows next to "Page 1 of 3" to move between pages.

If you have not performed any recorded action yet, you see "No audit entries yet — Activity you perform will appear here." If your filters match nothing, you see "No results match these filters." with **Clear filters**.

## Timeline Activities

For a quick look at your recent activity without leaving the page you are on, open the command palette and choose **View Timeline Activities**. A side panel lists your latest 100 entries, each with a short description and "Performed by *name* • *time ago*". Select **×** to close it.

## Retention

Audit entries are append-only: Crewflow cannot change or delete them.
