## The formula

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gross minutes">
    The minutes between the clock-in and clock-out times, rounded to the nearest minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subtract the break">
    The break recorded for that person, otherwise their individual break from the request, otherwise the shift's break. Worked minutes can never go below zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the rate">
    Worked minutes × the hourly rate saved on the person's place on the shift, rounded to the nearest penny.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Example

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Clock in / clock out | 16:58 – 23:04 → 366 minutes |
| Break | 30 minutes |
| Worked | 336 minutes (5.60 hours) |
| Rate | £15.00/hr |
| **Pay** | **£84.00** |

## Which rate

When a shift is created, Crewflow sets the shift's hourly rate:

1. the **Pay rate override** entered for the shift, if any, otherwise
2. the venue's **role rate** in effect on the shift date, otherwise
3. the job role's **default rate**.

When a person's place on the shift is confirmed, the shift's rate at that moment is saved on their place, and their pay is calculated from that saved rate. Changing a venue's role rates or a job role's default rate afterwards does **not** change shifts already created or pay already calculated.

## When pay is calculated

Hours and pay are calculated when the staff member clocks out, and recalculated if a venue manager corrects the times or the break. Finalising the timesheet does not change the amount.

## Rounding and money

All amounts are held in whole pence. Crewflow never stores pay as a decimal that could drift.

## What is not included

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Paid breaks">
    Venues have a **Paid** option for breaks, but the calculation currently always deducts the break. Pay for paid breaks must be added outside Crewflow for now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Overtime and premiums">
    No automatic overtime or premium rates. Time worked past the scheduled end is paid at the same rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Holiday pay, tax and deductions">
    Not calculated. Crewflow shows gross pay for hours worked only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missing clock-outs">
    An attendance with no clock-out has no worked time or pay until a venue manager adds the clock-out time with a reason. See [Late arrivals and missing clock-outs](/attendance/late-and-missing-clock-outs).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
