## The formula

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gross minutes">
    The minutes between the clock-in and clock-out times, rounded to the nearest minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subtract the break">
    The break recorded for that person, otherwise their individual break from the request, otherwise the shift's break. Worked minutes can never go below zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the rate">
    Worked minutes × the hourly rate that was locked in when the offer was sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Example

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Clock in / clock out | 16:58 – 23:04 → 366 minutes |
| Break | 30 minutes |
| Worked | 336 minutes (5.60 hours) |
| Rate | £15.00/hr |
| **Pay** | **£84.00** |

## Which rate

The rate comes from the shift, at the moment each offer is sent:

1. the shift's **Pay rate override**, if set
2. the venue's **role rate** for the shift date
3. the job role's **default rate**

That rate is saved on the person's assignment. Changing rates afterwards does **not** change offers already sent or pay already calculated.

## Rounding and money

All amounts are held in whole pence. Crewflow never stores pay as a decimal that could drift.

## What is not included

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Paid breaks">
    Venues have a **Paid** option for breaks, but the calculation currently always deducts the break. Pay for paid breaks must be added outside Crewflow for now.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Overtime and premiums">
    No automatic overtime or premium rates. Time worked past the scheduled end is paid at the same rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Holiday pay, tax and deductions">
    Not calculated. Crewflow shows gross pay for hours worked only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missing clock-outs">
    An attendance with no clock-out has no worked time until someone corrects it with a reason.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
