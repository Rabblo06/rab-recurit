The **Calendar** shows your workspace's shifts one month at a time, with the selected day's details beside it.

</Frame>

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs. Staff and venue managers have their own calendars in the mobile app.

## Find your way around

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Jumps to the current month and selects today. |
| **Previous month** / **Next month** | Moves a month at a time. |
| A day | Selects it and shows its shifts in the panel. |

Each day shows up to three shifts as "time · venue", with **+N more** when there are others. Weeks start on Monday, and times are shown in your timezone, as noted under the legend.

## The day panel

The panel shows the weekday, the date and "N shifts scheduled". Each shift card shows:

- the venue, role and time
- **x / y staff allocated**
- the shift status
- the names of confirmed staff

A day with nothing planned reads "A clear day ahead — Create a shift to start planning your team's coverage."

## Legend

**Schedule at a glance** explains the colours: Today, Selected day, Open / awaiting staff, Confirmed and Completed.

## Create a shift from the calendar

Select **New shift** at the top or **Add shift** in the day panel. The [New shift](/shifts/creating-shifts) drawer opens. Enter the date in the form — it is not filled in from the day you selected.

## What the calendar does not do

<Note>
  The calendar is a month view only. Day and week views, filters, bulk scheduling, and dragging a shift to another day are not available. To change a shift's time, cancel it and create a new one.
</Note>
