When a staff member accepts an offer, it waits for your confirmation before the

shift is booked. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

On Offers, open the view menu and choose Awaiting Confirmation.

Each row shows the staff member, the shift, and when they accepted.

To confirm one offer, select the tick on its row, check the details, then select

Confirm shift.

To confirm everyone else at once, select Confirm All Accepted.

Switch the view to Confirmed. Accepting and confirming are separate steps, and

only confirmed offers are booked.
