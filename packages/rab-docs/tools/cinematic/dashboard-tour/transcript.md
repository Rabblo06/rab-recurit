# The Dashboard

Here's your Crewflow dashboard. It brings your staff, offers and shifts together, so you can see how the week's going at a glance.

The strip along the top shows how your current offers are progressing: pending, accepted by staff, and confirmed.

Beside it are your totals for staff, venue managers and venues.

Hours worked adds up the time your team recorded over the last seven days.

Hover over a day to see its exact total.

Next to it, Shifts by venue counts the last thirty days of shifts at your busiest venues.

Shift readiness follows your next shift, from staffing and attendance through to the finalised timesheet.

Its checklist shows what's done, and what still needs attention.

Further down, the week view lists your shifts day by day. Use the arrows to look ahead to next week.

That's the dashboard. Select the arrow on any card to open the page behind it.
