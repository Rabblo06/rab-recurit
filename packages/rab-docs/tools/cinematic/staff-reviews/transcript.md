# Reading staff reviews

Staff reviews collect the feedback venue managers give your staff after a shift. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

Open Users, and select View on a staff member.

Open the More menu beside the tabs, and choose Staff reviews.

Overall rating shows the average score, and how the reviews are spread from one to five stars.

Average by category breaks the score down into grooming, attitude, skills, attendance and time keeping.

Use the venue manager and date range filters to narrow the reviews, for example to this month.

Review history lists each review. Select View review to see the full shift review.

The shift review shows each category score and any written feedback. Reviews are read only here.
