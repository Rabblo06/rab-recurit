## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Before you begin

- The [venue](/venues/overview) exists and is active.
- The staff you want to invite are **active** (they have accepted their invitation and are not deactivated).

## Create a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New shift">
    Go to **Shifts** and select **New shift**. You can also select **New shift** or **Add shift** in the **Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the venue and role">
    Search for the **Venue**. Choose the **Role**, or select **+ New role…** to create one with a **New role name** and **Default rate (£/hr)**. The **Address** fills in from the venue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and times">
    Enter the **Date**, **Start time** and **End time**, and the **Break (minutes)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rate (optional)">
    Leave **Pay rate override (£/hr)** empty to "Use venue/role rate", or enter a rate for this shift only. Add **Notes** if staff need extra information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the staff">
    Under **Staff members (select one or more)**, choose who should receive an offer. **Select all** and **Clear all** help with larger teams. Only active staff are listed.
    <Frame caption="The New shift drawer with a venue, role, times and three staff members selected.">
      <img src="/images/web/shifts/new-shift-drawer.webp" alt="New shift drawer with venue, role, date, start and end time, break and three selected staff members" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send Offer">
    The button shows how many offers will go out, for example **Send Offer (3)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Crewflow creates the shift and sends one offer to each person, then shows the result:

<Frame caption="“Shift created ✓ — 2 of 3 offers sent”: a tick for each offer sent, and a cross with the reason for anyone who could not be offered.">
  <img src="/images/web/shifts/new-shift-result.webp" alt="Shift created confirmation listing each staff member with a sent tick" />
</Frame>

- Each offer locks in the pay rate for that person.
- Staff see the offer in the mobile app, with the venue, time and estimated pay.
- Offers stay open for **48 hours**, then expire if unanswered.
- The shift appears in **Shifts** and the **Calendar** with the status **offered**.

If some offers could not be sent, the result lists who was missed and why — for example "This staff member already has a confirmed shift that overlaps this time." Crewflow never offers someone a shift that clashes with one they are already confirmed for. Send to them again with **Send offer** on the shift — see [Sending offers](/shifts/sending-offers).

| --- | --- |
| **Tabs** | All shifts, Upcoming, Completed |
| **Filter** | Venue, Status, Shift date, Job role |
| **Sort** | Shift date, Venue, Role, Status, Created date |
| **Columns** | Venue, Role, Date, Time, Filled (x / y), Rate, Status |

Hover over a row to show its action buttons, which depend on the shift's status: **Review request** (venue requests awaiting approval), **Publish** (drafts), **Send offer** (shifts with open places) and **Cancel shift**.

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Select a venue." / "Select or create a role." | Choose both before sending. |
| "Enter an address for this shift." | The venue had no address; type one. |
| "End time must be after start time." | Check the times. For an overnight shift, check the date and times carefully. |
| "Select at least one staff member." | Every new shift is created together with its first offers. |
