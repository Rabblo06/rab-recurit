Creating a shift and offering it to people is one action. Crewflow creates the shift and sends **one offer to each person you choose**.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Before you begin

- The [venue](/venues/overview) exists and is active. Archived venues are not listed.
- The staff you want to invite are **active**: they have accepted their invitation and are not deactivated. Only active staff appear in the staff picker.

## Create a shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open New shift">
    Go to **Shifts** and select **New shift**. You can also select **New shift** or **Add shift** in the [Calendar](/calendar/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the venue and role">
    Search for the **Venue**. Choose the **Role**, or select **+ New role…** and enter a **New role name** and **Default rate (£/hr)**. The **Address** fills in from the venue. You can change it for this shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and times">
    Enter the **Date**, **Start time** and **End time**, and the **Break (minutes)**. The start and end are on the same date, so the end time must be later than the start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rate (optional)">
    Leave **Pay rate override (£/hr)** empty to use the venue/role rate, or enter a rate for this shift only. See [Pay rates](/venues/pay-rates). Add **Notes** if staff need extra information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the staff">
    Under **Staff members**, select **Select staff**. The full-page **Select Staff** list opens, showing active staff only. Search by name, tick each person who should receive an offer, and select **Confirm**. **Cancel** returns to the drawer without changing your selection.

    Back in the drawer, the people you chose are listed with "N staff selected". Use the remove button next to a name to drop one person, **Clear all** to start again, or **Change staff** to reopen the list.
    <Frame caption="The New shift drawer with a venue, role, times and three staff members selected.">
      <img src="/images/web/shifts/new-shift-drawer.webp" alt="New shift drawer with venue, role, date, start and end time, break and three selected staff members" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send Offer">
    With more than one person selected, the button shows how many offers will go out, for example **Send Offer (3)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If someone has marked themselves unavailable

Staff can say which weekdays they are available. If anyone you selected is unavailable on the shift's day, Crewflow stops before sending anything and shows **Staff availability warning**:

- For one person: "*Name* has marked themselves as unavailable on *day*." and their **Available days**.
- For several people: "*X* of *Y* selected Staff have availability conflicts.", with each person's unavailable dates and available days. People who have not given their availability are not treated as unavailable.

Select **Cancel** to go back and change your selection, or **Send anyway** to send all the offers.

## What happens next

Crewflow creates the shift and sends one offer to each person.

- If every offer was sent, the drawer closes.
- If any offer could not be sent, the drawer stays open with a summary such as "Shift created ✓ — 2 of 3 offers sent", a tick for each offer sent, and a cross with the reason for anyone who was missed.

<Frame caption="“Shift created ✓ — 2 of 3 offers sent”: a tick for each offer sent, and a cross with the reason for anyone who could not be offered.">
  <img src="/images/web/shifts/new-shift-result.webp" alt="Shift created confirmation listing each staff member with a sent tick" />
</Frame>

After the shift is created:

- Staff see the offer in the mobile app, with the venue, time, hourly rate and estimated pay.
- Offers stay open for **48 hours**, then expire if unanswered.
- The shift appears in **Shifts** and the **Calendar** with the status **Offered**.
- When people accept, you confirm them on the [Offers](/shifts/confirming-offers) page.

Crewflow never offers someone a shift that clashes with one they are already confirmed for — for example "This staff member already has a confirmed shift that overlaps this time." To try someone again later, use **Send offer** on the shift. See [Sending offers](/shifts/sending-offers).

| Tool | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Tabs** | **All shifts** and **Completed** |
| **View menu** | **All shifts** or any single status, such as **Offered** or **Partially filled** |
| **Search** | Search the list |
| **Filter** | Venue, Status, Job role |
| **Sort** | Shift date, Venue, Role, Status, Created date |
| **Columns** | Venue, Role, Date, Time, Filled (x / y), Rate, Status. Show or hide columns from the options button. |

Hover over a row to show its action buttons. Which buttons appear depends on the shift's status:

| Button | Shown for |
| --- | --- |
| **Review request** | Staffing requests waiting for approval. You can also select the row. See [Venue requests](/shifts/venue-requests). |
| **Publish** | Draft shifts. |
| **Send offer** | Open, offered or partially filled shifts that still have places. |
| **Cancel shift** | Any shift that is not completed or cancelled, and is not a staffing request. |

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Select a venue." / "Select or create a role." | Choose both before sending. |
| "Enter an address for this shift." | The venue had no address. Type one. |
| "Select a date." / "Select a start time." / "Select an end time." | Fill in the date and both times. |
| "End time must be after start time." | Check the times. The start and end are on the same date. |
| "Select at least one staff member." | Every new shift is created together with its first offers. |
| "Could not create this shift. Check the details and try again." | Check the form and try again. |
