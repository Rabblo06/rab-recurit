When a staff member accepts, they are **not yet on the shift**. A manager confirms them. This lets you choose between people when more accept than you need.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

The view menu filters to **All offers**, **Pending Staff Response**, **Awaiting Confirmation**, **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn** or **Expired**. The **Status** column shows how long ago someone accepted, or the reason for a rejection.

## Confirm one person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show acceptances">
    Choose the **Awaiting Confirmation** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Confirm shift">
    Hover over the row and select the tick button. The drawer reads "*Name* has accepted this offer at *venue* on *date*…".
    <Frame caption="Confirm shift? — confirming makes the shift officially confirmed for this person.">
      <img src="/images/web/offers/confirm-offer-drawer.webp" alt="Confirm shift drawer describing the accepted offer" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The offer becomes **confirmed** and the staff member is told.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm everyone who accepted

In the **Awaiting Confirmation** view, select **Confirm All Accepted (N)**. Crewflow confirms every accepted offer in each batch.

<Frame caption="Confirm All Accepted confirms every acceptance in a batch at once.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/offers-awaiting-confirmation.webp" alt="Offers filtered to Awaiting Confirmation with the Confirm All Accepted button" />
</Frame>

To see everyone who received the same send, select **View batch (N recipients)** on the row. The batch drawer lets you confirm or reject each person and **View activity**.

<Frame caption="The batch drawer lists every recipient and their status.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/batch-drawer.webp" alt="Batch drawer listing recipients with Pending, Awaiting Confirmation and Confirmed statuses" />
</Frame>

## Reject an acceptance

Select **Reject**, optionally add a **Reason (optional)**, and choose **Reject offer**. The staff member is told the offer was not confirmed.

<Frame caption="Reject accepted offer? — the reason is optional.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/reject-offer-drawer.webp" alt="Reject accepted offer drawer with an optional reason field" />
</Frame>

## Automatic confirmation

| Shift came from | When staff accept |
| --- | --- |
| A **venue manager's request** you approved | Confirmed straight away — you already chose these people when you approved. |
| A shift **you created** | Waits for you. If nobody confirms or rejects within **60 minutes**, Crewflow confirms automatically and sends you "Shift auto-confirmed". |

Automatic confirmation never applies to a cancelled shift.

## What happens next

Confirmed staff see the shift under **Upcoming Shift** and in their calendar, receive reminders before it starts, and can clock in from 15 minutes before the start.
