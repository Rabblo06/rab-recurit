When a staff member accepts, they are **not yet on the shift**. Staff acceptance is the first step; **manager confirmation** is the second. Confirming lets you choose between people when more accept than you need.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

<VideoTutorial src="/videos/offers/confirm-offers.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/confirm-offers.webp" captions="/videos/captions/confirm-offers.en.vtt" title="Confirming accepted offers" duration="Under 1 minute" narrated />

<Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
  When a staff member accepts an offer, it waits for your confirmation before the shift is booked. This tutorial uses fictional demonstration data.

  On Offers, open the view menu and choose Awaiting Confirmation.

  Each row shows the staff member, the shift, and when they accepted.

  To confirm one offer, select the tick on its row, check the details, then select Confirm shift.

  To confirm everyone else at once, select Confirm All Accepted.

  Switch the view to Confirmed. Accepting and confirming are separate steps, and only confirmed offers are booked.
</Accordion>

## The Offers page

**Offers** has two tabs: **Staff Offers** (every offer you sent) and **Venue Offers** (staffing requests and their staffing boards). On **Staff Offers**, the view menu filters to **All**, **Pending Staff Response**, **Awaiting Confirmation**, **Confirmed**, **Declined**, **Rejected**, **Withdrawn** or **Expired**. **Expired** lists offers nobody answered before they ran out — they can no longer be accepted. **Filter** narrows by **Status**, **Shift date** or **Sent date**.

The **Status** column shows **Pending**, **Staff accepted** (with how long ago), **Manager confirmed**, **Manager rejected**, **Declined**, **Expired** or **Withdrawn**, and the reason where one was given.

## Confirm everyone who accepted

In the **Awaiting Confirmation** view, select **Confirm All Accepted (N)**. The button appears when the listed acceptances came from a batch send. Crewflow confirms **every accepted offer in each of those batches**, including any that are not on the current page.

<Frame caption="Confirm All Accepted confirms every acceptance in a batch at once.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/offers-awaiting-confirmation.webp" alt="Offers filtered to Awaiting Confirmation with the Confirm All Accepted button" />
</Frame>

### Batches

When two or more people on the current page received the same send, their rows show **View batch (N recipients)**. The batch drawer shows:

- the venue, role, date and time
- a count for each status, for example **Pending: 2** and **Awaiting Confirmation: 1**
- every recipient with their status, and **Confirm** and **Reject** buttons for anyone waiting for confirmation
- **View activity**, which lists what happened to that person's offer
- **Confirm All Accepted (N)** at the bottom, when anyone has accepted

## Reject an acceptance

Hover over the row and select **Reject**. The **Reject accepted offer?** drawer explains that "*Name* will be notified that their shift at *venue* on *date* was not confirmed." Optionally add a **Reason (optional)**, then select **Reject offer**. The status changes to **Manager rejected**.

Rejecting from the batch drawer does not ask for a reason.

## Automatic confirmation

| Shift came from | When staff accept |
| --- | --- |
| A **venue manager's staffing request** you approved | Confirmed straight away — you already chose these people when you approved. |
| A shift **you created** | Waits for you. If nobody confirms or rejects within **60 minutes**, Crewflow confirms automatically and sends you "Shift auto-confirmed". |

Automatic confirmation never applies to a cancelled shift. Offers can't be confirmed more than 5 hours after the shift's scheduled end.

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Could not confirm — the shift may already be full or this offer may have changed status." | Refresh the list. The shift may be full, or the offer may have been withdrawn or expired. |
| "This shift ended more than 5 hours ago, so this offer can no longer be confirmed." | The confirmation window has closed. |
| "Could not reject this offer. Try again." | Refresh and try again. |

## What happens next

Confirmed staff see the shift under **Upcoming Shift** and in their calendar, receive reminders before it starts, and can clock in from 15 minutes before the start.
