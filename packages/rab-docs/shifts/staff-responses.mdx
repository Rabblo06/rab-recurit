## Accepting is not the same as being confirmed

A staff member accepting an offer is the **first** of two steps:

| Step | Who | Offer status | What the staff member sees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Staff acceptance** | The staff member taps **Accept** | Staff accepted | "Awaiting manager confirmation" |
| **Manager confirmation** | The manager who sent the offer confirms it. Crewflow never confirms automatically. | Manager confirmed | **CONFIRMED** |

Only after the second step is the person on the shift and able to clock in.

## Accepting

The staff member opens the offer and taps **Accept**. On your Offers page the row shows a **Staff accepted** status with "Staff accepted *N minutes ago*" underneath. The offer is listed in the **Awaiting Confirmation** view until it is confirmed or rejected.

Acceptance only succeeds if the offer is still pending and has not passed its deadline. A standard offer can't be accepted less than 30 minutes before its start: the staff member sees "This shift starts too soon for a manager to confirm it, so it can no longer be accepted." and the offer stays pending until it expires. If two things happen at once — for example you withdraw an offer as they tap Accept — only one of them wins.

## Declining

The staff member taps **Decline**. The offer becomes **Declined** and cannot be reopened. You are notified, and the place can be offered to someone else — see [Sending offers](/shifts/sending-offers).

The staff app does not ask for a reason when declining. If a reason is recorded on an offer, it appears under its status on the Offers page.

## No answer

If the staff member does nothing, the offer expires at its deadline (48 hours after sending, by default). Expired offers are listed in the **Expired** view on the Offers page. See [Offer expiry](/shifts/offer-expiry).

## After the manager decides

| Manager action | Staff member sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm shift** | **CONFIRMED**. The shift appears under Upcoming Shift and in their calendar. |
| **Reject** | The offer ends. It no longer appears as an upcoming shift. |
| No confirmation by the approval deadline (30 minutes before the start for a standard offer) | **Withdrawn**, with "Shift offer withdrawn". It never becomes confirmed. This applies to approved staffing requests too. |
