Venue managers ask for staff from the mobile app's **Send Shift** screen. This is a **staffing request**, not an offer: it reaches you as a shift with the status **Pending approval**, and nothing is sent to staff until you approve it. When you approve, Crewflow sends **one offer to each selected person**. You then follow those offers on the request's live staffing board.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs who can approve staffing requests.

## Find requests

Select **Venue Offers** in the sidebar, under **Operations**, or open **Offers** and select the **Venue Offers** tab. You are also notified when a request is submitted.

<Frame caption="Offers → Venue Offers lists every request with its staffing and status.">
  <img src="/images/web/offers/venue-offers-list.webp" alt="Venue Offers list with venue, role, venue manager, date, staff selected and status" />
</Frame>

The view menu shows **All requests**, **Pending**, **Approved** or **Declined**. The list refreshes every few seconds.

By default the columns are **Venue**, **Role**, **Date**, **Staff** and **Status**. You can also show **Note**, **Venue Manager**, **Time**, **Rate** and **Submitted** from the options button. The **Staff** column shows how many people were selected against how many are required.

The **Status** column describes the request as a whole:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending approval** | Waiting for a manager's decision. |
| **Declined** | A manager declined the request. |
| **Offered** | Approved; offers are out and nobody has accepted yet. |
| **Partially filled** | Approved; some people have accepted or been confirmed. |
| **Fully filled** | Approved; every required place is confirmed. |
| ***N* staff rejected** | Approved; *N* people declined their offer. |

Selecting a row opens the request. Pending and declined requests open the **Shift Approval** drawer. Approved requests open their [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).

## Review and approve

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the request">
    Select a pending request to open the **Shift Approval** drawer. It shows the venue, **Role**, **Status**, **Date**, **Time**, **Rate**, **Break**, **Staffing** ("x selected / y required (z open)"), the venue manager's **Note** and the **Selected staff**. Each selected person shows their break, their own start and end times if the venue manager set them, and their email.
    <Frame caption="The Shift Approval drawer.">
      <img src="/images/web/offers/shift-approval-drawer.webp" alt="Shift approval drawer with staffing summary, note and selected staff list" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the staff (optional)">
    Select the remove button next to anyone who should not be offered the shift. People marked **No longer active** or **Unavailable for shift** can't be offered, so remove them and choose someone else.

    To add people, select **Select N Staff** (N is the number of open places). The **Select Staff** page lists active staff, shows "x of y selected · N remaining", and won't let you tick more people than the shift needs or anyone unavailable that day. Select **Confirm** to save the selection to the request, or **Cancel** to discard your changes. When every place is filled the button reads **Staff Complete ✓**.
    <Frame caption="Select Staff: choose people to fill the remaining places.">
      <img src="/images/web/offers/select-staff-page.webp" alt="Select Staff page listing staff with name, reference, email, rate and status" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Select **Approve**. You need at least one selected person, and no more than the number required. If anyone selected is unavailable on that day, you see the **Staff availability warning** first. Choose **Cancel** or **Send anyway**.

    Crewflow sends one offer to each selected person and opens the request's [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline). When people accept, they are **confirmed automatically**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decline a request

Select **Decline**, optionally add a **Reason (optional)**, and select **Confirm decline**. Select **Back** if you change your mind. The venue manager is told the request was declined. A declined request still opens in the **Shift Approval** drawer, read-only, with its **Decline reason**.

## Notifications

| Event | Who is told |
| --- | --- |
| Request submitted | Managers who approve requests |
| Request approved or declined | The venue manager who sent it |
| You removed someone from the selection | The venue manager, so they know their choice changed |

## If something goes wrong

- "Could not approve this request. It may already have been actioned." or "Could not decline this request. It may already have been actioned." — another manager may have dealt with it. Refresh **Venue Offers**.
- "Could not save this selection." — staff eligibility or the request changed. Select **Cancel**, reopen the request and try again.

## After approval

Follow each person from offer to clock-out on the [live staffing board](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).
