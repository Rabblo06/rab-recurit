Each approved staffing request has a **live staffing** board. It shows where every person is, from the moment their offer is sent until their attendance is reviewed. The board refreshes every few seconds without reloading the page.

## Who can use this

The manager who approved the request. The board is for approved staffing requests only. Shifts you created yourself are followed on the [Offers](/shifts/confirming-offers) page.

  Open Venue Offers in the sidebar, and select a shift.

Go to **Venue Offers** (in the sidebar under **Operations**, or **Offers → Venue Offers**) and select an approved request. Approving a request also opens its board. **← Venue Offers** takes you back.

The top bar shows the venue name, a status for the whole request (for example **Partially filled** or **Fully filled**), and the date and time.

## Summary

Across the top are two groups of counts:

- **Seats:** **Required**, **Filled** (confirmed places) and **Open**.
- **Staff by stage:** one count for each column — **Offered**, **Waiting**, **Accepted**, **Confirmed**, **Late**, **Clocked in**, **Pending Review**, **Clocked out**, **No Show**, **Rejected**, **Cancelled** and **Deleted**. Each count always matches the number of cards in its column. Select a count to scroll the board to that column.

## Columns

The board has 12 columns, always in this order. Each person appears in exactly one column, based on their latest offer for this shift. Columns can't be dragged — a card moves when something actually happens, such as an acceptance or a clock-in.

| # | Column | A person is here when… | Card shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **OFFERED** | Their offer was sent and they have not opened it yet. | "Offer sent" |
| 2 | **WAITING** | They have opened the offer but not answered. | "Notification read - awaiting response" |
| 3 | **STAFF ACCEPTED** | They accepted and are waiting for confirmation. | "Accepted - *time* - awaiting confirmation" |
| 4 | **CONFIRMED** | They are confirmed for the shift. | "Confirmed - starts *time*" |
| 5 | **LATE STAFF** | They are confirmed but have not clocked in 10 minutes after the start. | "Not clocked in - start *time*" |
| 6 | **CLOCKED IN** | They have clocked in. | "Clocked in - *time*" |
| 7 | **PENDING REVIEW** | Their attendance needs the venue manager's review — a missing clock-out, a dispute, or a corrected time. | "Clock-out missing - in *time*", "Attendance disputed - awaiting review" or "Corrected - out *time* - awaiting review" |
| 8 | **CLOCKED OUT** | They have clocked out. | "Clocked out - *time*", with a **Reviewed** or **Awaiting review** tag |
| 9 | **NO SHOW** | They were confirmed but never clocked in. | "No clock-in recorded - no-show" |
| 10 | **REJECTED** | They declined the offer, or a manager rejected their acceptance. | Who ended it, for example "Declined by staff" or "Rejected by manager" |
| 11 | **CANCELLED** | Their booking or the whole shift was cancelled. | For example "Booking cancelled", "Shift cancelled" or "Cancelled by Internal Manager" |
| 12 | **DELETED OFFER** | The offer expired or was withdrawn before it was accepted. | For example "Offer expired", "Offer withdrawn" or "Cancelled by Internal Manager" |

Cards in **REJECTED**, **CANCELLED** and **DELETED OFFER** also show any reason that was given, and when the offer ended. Other cards show when the offer was sent, the shift times, or — once the person has clocked out — their clock-in, clock-out, break and worked time. If someone has been offered this shift more than once, the card notes "*N* earlier offers".

<Note>
  The live staffing board is read-only for attendance. Correcting times, marking attendance as reviewed and finalising the timesheet are done by the venue manager in the mobile app. See [Timesheet review](/attendance/timesheet-review).
</Note>

## Card actions

Open the menu on a person's card for **View staff**, **Notes** and **Cancel offer**.

**Cancel offer** asks "Cancel *name*'s offer?" with **Keep Staff** or **Cancel Offer**. You can cancel up to **15 minutes before the shift starts**, as long as the person has not clocked in.

## Fill gaps

Select **+ Select replacement staff** to open **Select Staff** in replacement mode. Choose people and select **Send offers**. They join the board in **OFFERED**.

## Timesheets

Select **Timesheet report** in the top bar. Until every staff member has clocked out, Crewflow shows "Timesheet is not ready yet! Wait for all staff to clock out" and nothing opens. After that, the read-only **Timesheet Report** drawer opens:

| Document | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Original Timesheet** | The times as recorded, before review. Marked "Unsigned". |
| **Venue Signed Timesheet** | The version after the venue manager reviewed and finalised it. |

Each has **View** and **Download**.
