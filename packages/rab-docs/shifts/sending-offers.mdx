An offer invites **one person** to one shift. You can add more people to a shift that is already live at any time while it still has open places.

## Who can use this

Managers and CEOs.

## Send more offers to an existing shift

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the shift">
    In **Shifts**, hover over a shift that is open, offered or partially filled and still has places.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Send offer">
    The **Send offer** drawer shows the venue, role, date and time, and how many seats are open — for example "Riverside House · Bartender · 24 Oct 2026, 17:00–23:00 · 2 seats open".
    <Frame caption="Send offers to an existing shift.">
      <img src="/images/web/shifts/send-offers-drawer.webp" alt="Send offers drawer with shift summary, seats open and staff selection" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the staff">
    Under **Staff members**, select **Select staff**. On the **Select Staff** page, tick the people you want and select **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send offer**, or **Send offers (N)** when you have chosen more than one person. If anyone is unavailable on that day, you see the **Staff availability warning** first. Choose **Cancel** or **Send anyway**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Afterwards, the drawer lists each person with a tick if their offer was sent, or a cross and the reason if it was not. If every offer was sent, the drawer closes. Otherwise select **Done** when you have read the result.

## Sending to many people

You can send up to 100 offers in one go, whether you are creating a shift or adding to one. Each send is grouped as a **batch**, so later you can confirm everyone who accepted from that batch together.

It is common to offer more people than you need. The shift fills as people accept and are confirmed. When the shift is full, confirm the people you want and withdraw any remaining pending offers.

## Withdraw an offer

On the **Offers** page, hover over a pending offer and select **Withdraw offer**. The drawer explains: "The staff member will no longer be able to accept this offer." Select **Withdraw offer** to confirm, or **Back**.

Only offers that are still waiting for the staff member's answer can be withdrawn.

## Replacing someone who drops out

When an offer is declined or expires, Crewflow lets you know that a place needs filling. Crewflow never sends a replacement offer by itself — you always choose who receives it.

To fill the place:

- On the **Shifts** page, select **Send offer** on the shift and choose a new person, **or**
- For an approved staffing request, open its live staffing board, select **+ Select replacement staff**, choose people and select **Send offers**. See [Live staffing](/shifts/live-staffing-pipeline).

## Common errors

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Could not send these offers. Try again." | Try again. If it keeps failing, reopen the shift. |
| "This shift ended more than 5 hours ago, so no new offers can be sent for it." | Offers can't be sent for a shift that ended over 5 hours ago. |
| A cross next to a person's name | Read the reason shown. For example, the person may already have an overlapping confirmed shift. |

## What the staff member sees

The offer appears in their app with the role, venue, address, date and time, hourly rate and estimated pay for the shift. See [Staff responses](/shifts/staff-responses).
