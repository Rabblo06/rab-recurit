## Where notifications appear

| Channel | Status | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **In-app** | Available | Web console: the bell in the top bar. Mobile app: the bell on Home and **Profile → Inbox**. |
| **Email** | Available | Invitations, password emails, timesheet PDFs, and notifications you choose to receive by email. |
| **Push (phone lock screen)** | In development | Built for Android but not yet verified on devices. Not available on iOS. |

## Use the bell in the web console

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    Select the bell (**Notifications**) in the top bar. A red badge shows how many are unread, up to "9+".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read">
    The **Notifications** list shows your most recent notifications, newest first, each with a title, a message and how long ago it arrived. Unread ones are highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as read">
    Select a notification to mark it read, or select **Mark all read** (shown while you have unread notifications).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The web console checks for new notifications every 30 seconds. With nothing to show, the list reads "No notifications yet". Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click elsewhere to close it.

## What you are told about

| Event | Usually sent to |
| --- | --- |
| Offer sent | The staff member |
| Offer accepted, declined or expired | The manager who sent it |
| Offer confirmed or rejected | The staff member |
| Shift auto-confirmed | The responsible manager |
| Shift request submitted | Managers who approve requests |
| Shift request approved, declined, or staff removed | The venue manager |
| Shift reminders — 24 hours, 2 hours and 30 minutes before | Confirmed staff |
| Clock-in is open | Confirmed staff, 15 minutes before the start |
| Late clock-in, no-show, missing clock-out | Managers |
| Replacement required | The manager, after a decline or expiry |
| Shift cancelled | Everyone with an offer or place on the shift |

## Choose how you are notified

In **Settings → Account**, the **Notifications** section ("Manage how and when you receive notifications.") lists notification types, each with an **In-app** and an **Email** checkbox. Changes save as soon as you tick or untick a box; if a change cannot be saved you see "Could not save that preference."

The offer events are listed as:

- An offer is sent to me
- An offer I sent expires unanswered
- A staff member accepts my offer
- A staff member declines my offer
- An offer I accepted is confirmed
- An offer I accepted is rejected

Until you change a type, it uses the defaults: **in-app on, email off**. See [Profile and preferences](/account/profile-and-preferences#notification-preferences).

<Note>
  Staff and venue managers cannot change notification preferences in the mobile app.
</Note>

## Suspended organisations

While an organisation is suspended, Crewflow sends it no notifications or emails.
