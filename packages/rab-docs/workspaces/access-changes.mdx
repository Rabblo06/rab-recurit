## Choose the right action

| Situation | Action | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invited by mistake, not yet active | **Cancel invitation** | The person's record in **Users**, or the **×** (Revoke) button in Members → Pending invitations |
| Staff member leaving or on long leave | **Deactivate** | Users → Staff → row actions or **More actions** |
| Manager or venue manager leaving | **Suspend** | The person's record in **Users** (Managers or Venue managers tab) → **More actions** |
| Workspace member leaving | **Suspend** | Settings → Members → Team. Select **Reinstate** to give access back. |
| Coming back | **Reactivate** | The same menus |
| Account created in error, no work history | **Delete user** | **More actions** → type DELETE and confirm with your password |

## What happens to their access

- **Deactivating or suspending** signs the person out on every device straight away. They cannot sign in until reactivated.
- **Cancelling an invitation** stops the invitation link working immediately.
- **Reactivating** lets them sign in again with their existing password.

## What happens to their records

Shifts, offers, attendance and timesheets stay exactly as they were. Pay already calculated is unchanged. This is why Crewflow will not permanently delete anyone with work history: "This user has historical workforce records and cannot be permanently deleted. Suspend the account instead."

## Moving someone to another workspace

Not supported. Each account belongs to one workspace. To have someone work for a different manager, that manager invites them with a different email address.

## Switching workspaces

Not supported. Each account works in exactly one workspace, which Crewflow chooses from your account at sign-in.
