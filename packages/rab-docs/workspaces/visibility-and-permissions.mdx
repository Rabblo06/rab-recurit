description: "Who can see which staff, venues and shifts inside a workspace."

---

What you can see depends on two things: your **workspace** and your **role**.

## Staff visibility

| Account | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Manager who owns the workspace | Every staff member in the workspace. |
| CEO | The staff in their own workspace. |
| Workspace member | What their custom role allows: the objects, fields and record filters set in **Settings → Members → Roles**. View only. |
| Venue manager | Only the staff the workspace manager has assigned to them (**Users → Venue managers → Assigned staff**), in the mobile app. Pay rates and internal records are not shown. |
| Staff | Only themselves. |

## Venue visibility

| Account | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Manager | Every venue in the workspace, active and archived. |
| Venue manager | Only the venues they are assigned to. |
| Staff | Only the venue name, address and notes on offers and shifts they are part of. |

## Shifts, offers and attendance

- Managers see every shift, offer and attendance record in their workspace.
- Venue managers see shifts, staffing and attendance for their own venues.
- Staff see their own offers, upcoming shifts and history.

## When something is out of scope

If you open a link to a record outside your workspace or venues, Crewflow responds with "not found". It does not reveal whether the record exists. If you can see a record but your role does not allow an action, the action is refused.
