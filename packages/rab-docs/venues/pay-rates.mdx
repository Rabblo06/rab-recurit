A venue can pay a role more or less than its usual rate — a bartender at a hotel event might earn more than at a restaurant.

## Add a role rate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the venue">
    In **Venues**, select **Edit** on the venue. (Rates can only be added after the venue is saved: "Save the venue first to add role pay rates.")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rate">
    Under **Pay Details**, choose the **Role** and enter the **Hourly rate (£)**. Select **+ Add another role rate** for more.
    <Frame caption="Pay Details with two role rates.">
      <img src="/images/web/venues/venue-role-rates.webp" alt="Edit venue drawer with Pay Details listing role and hourly rate rows" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Select **Save changes**. Use **Remove** to delete a rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which rate is used

When you create a shift, Crewflow picks its rate in this order:

1. The **Pay rate override (£/hr)** you enter on the shift.
2. The venue's **role rate** for that role, if one applies on the shift date.
3. The job role's **default rate**.

A shift takes its rate when it is created, and the rate is saved on each person's place when you confirm them. Changing a venue or role rate later never changes existing shifts, confirmed places or pay already calculated.

<Note>
  All amounts are held to the penny — Crewflow never rounds pay through decimals.
</Note>
