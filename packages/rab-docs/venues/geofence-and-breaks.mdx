## Location and geofence

A **geofence** is a circle around the venue. When it is enforced, staff can only clock in or out from inside the circle.

| Setting | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Latitude** / **Longitude** | The venue's position. Paste "lat, lng" from a map into Latitude to fill both. |
| **Geofence radius** | In metres. Default 200, minimum 50. |
| **Enforce geofence** | "When on, Staff can only clock in or out inside the radius. Off by default." |

When enforcement is on:

- The staff member's phone must report a location accurate to within 100 metres. A less accurate reading is refused with "We couldn't confirm your location accurately enough. Please try again."
- Clocking in from outside the circle is refused with "You need to be at *venue* to clock in."
- If a staff member leaves the area during a live shift while the app is open, Crewflow can clock them out automatically after checking the distance on the server.

When enforcement is off, staff still scan the shift QR code to clock in; their location is not required to match.

<Tip>
  Choose a radius that covers the entrance and staff areas. Large buildings or poor GPS reception indoors may need 200–300 metres.
</Tip>

## Break settings

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Default break minutes** | The break new shifts at this venue start with. You can change it on each shift, and venue managers can change it for individual staff. |
| **Paid** | Recorded on the venue. |

<Warning>
  Pay calculations currently **always deduct the break**, even when **Paid** is ticked. Until this changes, treat every break as unpaid. See [Pay calculation](/payroll/pay-calculation).
</Warning>
