A **venue manager** looks after one or more venues — for example, the events manager at a hotel. They use the mobile app in Venue manager mode.

## Who can use this

- **Managers** add and manage the venue managers in their own workspace. The manager who owns the workspace chooses which staff each venue manager can see.
- **CEOs** create venue managers from **Users → Managers**. See [Manager accounts](/account/manager-accounts).

## Add a venue manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Users**, select the **Venue managers** tab, then **New venue manager**. You can also open the account menu (your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and select **Add venue manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personal Details">
    Enter **First name** and **Last name**. Optionally select the avatar to add a photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Role">
    Add an optional **Job title**, then choose the venue in **Select Venue** (required).
  </Step>
  <Step title="General">
    Enter the **Email** and **Mobile number**. An invitation email is sent to this address, and they set their own password to activate the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Create venue manager">
    Crewflow creates the account, queues the invitation and opens their details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Invite member** in the account menu (Settings → Members → Invite) is for workspace members with a custom role, not for venue managers.
</Note>

## Your venue managers

**Users → Venue managers** lists the venue managers in your workspace with their **Name**, **Email**, **Venue**, **Status** and when they were **Added**. Search by name or email. Tick rows to use **Activate**, **Deactivate**, **Send Email** or **Delete** for several at once.

Select a name to open their details. The header shows "Venue manager" and their venues.

  <img src="/images/web/staff/venue-manager-detail.webp" alt="Venue manager detail panel with photo, Key Information and Personal Details" />

- **Overview** shows their photo (select it to change it, or **Remove photo**), **Key Information** (**Manager type**, **Venue**, **Job title**, **Status**) and **Personal Details**, including email and phone. Hover over a field and select the pencil to edit it.
- **Timeline** and **Email** work as they do for staff. **+2 More** holds **Notes** and **Assigned staff**.
- **More actions** (⋮) at the bottom offers the invitation actions while they have not activated, or **Reset password**, **Suspend** / **Reactivate** and **Delete user** once they have. See [Account states](/staff/account-states).

## Choose which staff a venue manager can see

<VideoTutorial src="/videos/managers/assign-venue-manager-staff.mp4" poster="/videos/posters/assign-venue-manager-staff.webp" captions="/videos/captions/assign-venue-manager-staff.en.vtt" title="Assigning staff to a venue manager" duration="Under 1 minute" narrated />

<Accordion title="Video transcript" icon="closed-captioning">
  A venue manager can only see and select the staff you assign to them. This tutorial shows how to assign a team, using fictional demonstration data.

  Open Users, then select the Venue managers tab.

  Select View on a venue manager to open their details.

  Open the More menu beside the tabs, and choose Assigned staff.

  This list is everyone the venue manager can currently see and select in their app.

  Select Add Staff. The Select Staff page lists the staff in your workspace, and anyone already assigned is marked Assigned.

  Tick the people you want to add, then select Confirm.

  They now appear in the assigned list. To remove someone, select the cross beside their name and confirm. Their account and work history are not deleted.
</Accordion>

A venue manager only sees the staff you assign to them. New staff are not visible to any venue manager until you add them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Assigned staff">
    Open the venue manager's details, select **+2 More**, then **Assigned staff**. The section shows **Assigned Staff** and how many people are on their team.
    <Frame caption="The Assigned staff section.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/venue-manager-assigned-staff.webp" alt="Assigned Staff list for a venue manager with search box and a remove button on each row" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add Staff">
    **Add Staff** is at the bottom of the panel. The **Select Staff** page opens, listing the staff in your workspace that can be assigned. People already on the team are ticked and marked **Assigned**.
    <Frame caption="Select Staff for a venue manager.">
      <img src="/images/web/staff/venue-manager-staff-picker.webp" alt="Full-page Select Staff list with checkboxes, already-assigned staff ticked, and Cancel and Confirm buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the people to add">
    Use **Search staff...** to find people. The page shows how many you have selected and how many are already assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Confirm">
    Crewflow adds them to the team and confirms, for example "2 staff members assigned." Select **Cancel** to leave without changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each person on the team shows their role, employment status and **Assigned**. Someone who has not signed in yet shows **Visible after sign-in**.

**Remove someone from the team:** select the **✕** on their row, then **Remove**. They no longer appear in the venue manager's app and cannot be chosen for new requests. Their staff account and work history are not deleted.

Assigning staff creates no account, offer or shift, and sends nothing to the staff member. Only active staff and staff who are still pending compliance can be assigned.

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "No staff assigned yet. … cannot see or select anyone until you add staff." | The team is empty. Select **Add Staff**. |
| "No assignable staff" | There are no staff you can assign. Create staff in **Users** first. |
| "Only the workspace owner can manage this venue manager's staff." | Your account cannot change this team. Ask the owner of the workspace. |
| "Could not assign the selected staff. Their status may have changed — Cancel and try again." | Someone's status changed. Select **Cancel**, then try again. |

## What venue managers can do

| Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- |
| See the venues they are assigned to | See other venues or the rest of the workspace |
| See the staff assigned to them, and mark their own favourites and emergency cover | See unassigned staff, staff pay rates or private details |
| Submit staffing requests with chosen staff, times and breaks | Send offers directly, or confirm and reject acceptances |
| Follow events: required, confirmed and open positions | Create or edit venues |
| Review attendance, correct times with a reason, and finalise timesheets | See payroll |
| | Sign in to the web console, or invite anyone |

## Their team in the mobile app

In the mobile app, the venue manager's **Users** screen lists their assigned team. They can filter it to **Favourite** or **Emergency** staff they have marked themselves. See [Your staff](/mobile/venue-manager-staff).
